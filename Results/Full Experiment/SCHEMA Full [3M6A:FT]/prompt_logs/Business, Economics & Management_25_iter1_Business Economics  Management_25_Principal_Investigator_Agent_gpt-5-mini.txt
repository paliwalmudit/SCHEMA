Primary specification: conditional logit with firm fixed effects, controls = lagged size, lagged ROA, prior OFDI experience, firm age, patent counts, industry×year and country×year fixed effects; clustering = country×industry; standard errors via wild-cluster bootstrap when clusters are few. Instrument construction examples: (1) policy_timing × industry_export_exposure (continuous) where industry_export_exposure = industry outbound intensity; (2) index_inclusion_dummy (announcement vs effective date) for provider rebalancing; (3) ETS_enactment_dummy. First stage: ESG_it = α + π Instruments_it + X_it-1 + FE + u_it; report F-stat and partial R2, Kleibergen–Paap. Second stage (2SRI): include first-stage residuals in conditional logit and report marginal effects with bootstrap CIs. Selection: Heckman two-step using pre-entry covariates and pre-treatment instruments for entry stage. TFP: estimate ACF residuals, generate persistent component via AR(1)/Kalman smoothing, use lagged persistent TFP (t-1) for ESG×TFP interaction. Ablations: (i) replace ACF with LP and OP; (ii) use provider-specific ESG instead of harmonized composite; (iii) no-IV vs IV; (iv) SIMEX correction vs raw; (v) alternate nonlinear IVs: control-function vs full-information structural mixed logit. Robustness and falsification: event-studies (CS-did, Sun & Abraham), announcement-placebo windows, placebo outcomes (pre-policy CAPEX), negative-control sectors, and leave-one-out network exposure. Evaluation metrics: average treatment effect on probability of greenfield (marginal effect), ATT from DiD, first-stage F-stat, Kleibergen–Paap, Anderson–Rubin/CLR p-values, mediated proportion attributed to TFP, and coverage/power metrics from Monte Carlo.",
  "Risk Factors and Limitations": "Data quality and coverage: ESG providers disagree and contain missing or nonrandomly missing entries (MNAR), limiting generalizability. Measurement error: attenuation in ESG and TFP estimates may bias interaction coefficients; SIMEX and rank approaches may not fully correct nonclassical errors. Instrument validity: policy timing or index events may violate exclusion (concurrent reforms, anticipation) producing biased IV estimates; weak instruments are possible, especially for subgroups. Nonlinear IV identification: 2SRI may be inconsistent under certain latent-utility specifications; full-information structural estimation is computationally heavy and may not converge. Selection and sample size: few treated clusters or low counts of greenfield events can reduce power and invalidate asymptotic inference. Spillovers and network dependence: unmodeled cross-firm spillovers can bias ATT estimates. External validity: results from Chinese listed firms may not generalize to privately held firms or other countries. Mediation limits: causal mediation relies on strong sequential‑ignorability or valid mediator instruments; otherwise mediation estimates are partially identified."
