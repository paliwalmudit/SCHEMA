  "Abstract": "This study maps dimensions of marital compatibility among married Libyan women in Tripoli and tests whether compatibility varies by family type, marriage type, spousal age gap, marriage length, and spouse-selection criteria. We will develop and culturally adapt a multi-domain marital compatibility instrument (communication, role negotiation, intimacy, extended-family interference, religious concordance, economic stress), validate it with item response theory (IRT) and confirmatory factor analysis (CFA), and test structural associations and mediation (e.g., communication → compatibility, mediated by perceived fairness) using structural equation modeling (SEM). Main contributions are a culturally grounded, psychometrically validated latent marital compatibility scale for Tripoli, transparent DIF/invariance analysis across subgroups, and reproducible power/simulation outputs that incorporate clustering and attrition. Expected outcomes include defensible measurement tools, pre-registered analytic rules for DIF and missing data, and empirically supported sample targets for future studies, producing evidence to inform culturally appropriate interventions and services for married women in Libya.
