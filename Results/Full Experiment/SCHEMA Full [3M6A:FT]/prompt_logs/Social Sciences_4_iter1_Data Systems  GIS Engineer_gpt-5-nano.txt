Voucher modalities
- Value and dosing

  - Expire vouchers monthly with a short grace period to encourage timely use and reduce stockpiling.
- Delivery modality
  - Digital vouchers via mobile money wallets or QR codes linked to participant IDs; offline paper vouchers as a contingency in low-connectivity areas.
