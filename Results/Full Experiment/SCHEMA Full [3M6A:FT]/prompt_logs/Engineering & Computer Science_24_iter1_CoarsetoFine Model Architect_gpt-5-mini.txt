1) High-level design overview:
- Two-stage coarse-to-fine pipeline: a Coarse Global Detector (CGD) produces a low-resolution but context-aware crack likelihood map; a Fine Refinement Module (FRM) takes high-resolution encoder features plus the upsampled coarse cue to predict crisp crack masks and skeleton/edge cues. This decouples global localization from local delineation and helps recover thin, disconnected crack patterns.

2) Core modules and design choices:
- Encoder: ResNet-50 or HRNet backbone with multi-scale feature pyramid outputs (C2–C5). Optionally a lightweight variant (MobileNetV3) for edge devices.
- Coarse Global Detector: FPN-style fusion with large receptive fields (dilated convs or atrous spatial pyramid pooling) to aggregate context and suppress background texture false positives. Output at 1/8–1/16 resolution.
- Cue Fusion Block: Cross-scale attention that modulates fine features by the coarse cue. Implement spatial attention (coarse map as guidance), channel attention (SE/CBAM), and gated convolution to selectively enhance crack-supportive features.
- Fine Refinement Module: Multi-branch local context extractor (small kernels + asymmetric convs) followed by a boundary-aware decoder predicting three outputs: mask, skeleton (thin centerline), and edge/confidence. Multi-task supervision helps thin structure recovery.
- Optional iterative refinement: one or two lightweight refinement steps where the refined mask re-enters FRM for further correction.

3) Losses and supervision:
- Class imbalance handling: combine weighted BCE/focal loss with soft Dice or IoU loss on mask prediction.
- Boundary/skeleton losses: binary BCE + L1 distance transform loss for centerline alignment. Add Hausdorff or boundary F1 loss if available.
- Consistency loss: enforce agreement between coarse cue and aggregated fine prediction (e.g., KL or L2 on downsampled maps).

- Baselines: single-stage UNet and FPN-based detector.
