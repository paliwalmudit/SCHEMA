Physiological preprocessing will standardize processing pipelines: ECG at 1 kHz, filtered 0.5–40 Hz, with RMSSD calculated in 10 s sliding windows; pupil data corrected for blinks and baseline normalized in a −500 to −200 ms pre-cue interval; EMG recorded from grip-related and facial muscles bandpass filtered (20–450 Hz), notch filtered at line noise frequencies, rectified, and enveloped. Motion monitoring will include real-time fMRI framewise displacement thresholds (mean FD <0.2 mm, volume censor at FD >0.5 mm), excluding runs exceeding 10% censored volumes.

Pilot feasibility studies (N=10) will validate the protocol, targeting go/no-go criteria for motion control, trialwise single-trial beta ICC ≥0.40, EEG regressor reliability ≥0.30, and participant tolerability ≤7/10 discomfort. Failures will mandate iterative refinement before full data collection. All code, scripts, analysis plans, and simulated datasets will be shared openly with clear provenance via containerized pipelines (Docker/Singularity) and BIDS-compliant data standards, with de-identification procedures thoroughly documented for physiological data.

Deliverables are assigned with a two-week deadline for pilot scripts, adaptive staircase code, and simulation repositories (Owners: Modeler, Statistician, Data Scientist) and an eight-week deadline for finalized preregistration texts, containerized pipelines, IRB and safety packages, and TMS protocols (Owners: PI, Safety Lead, TMS Lead, Data Scientist). This rigorous, transparent approach ensures reproducibility, statistical validity, and maximal scientific impact for our investigation of the dynamics of experienced effort.
