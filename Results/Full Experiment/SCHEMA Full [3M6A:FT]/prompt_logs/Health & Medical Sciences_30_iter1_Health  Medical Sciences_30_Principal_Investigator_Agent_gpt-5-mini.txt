Primary perturbations: (A) inducible Cas9 (heat-shock or drug-inducible promoter) targeting tp53 in neurons; (B) UV-C exposure (254 nm) graded doses; (C) bleomycin exposure graded concentrations. Dose ranges: pilot titration per species to identify nonlethal behaviorally tolerable range (expected pilot groups: 5 dose levels plus sham). Baseline cohorts and controls: sham induction, non-targeting sgRNA, heat-shock only, and melatonin treatment as pharmacological control. Imaging: two-photon microscopy with pan-neuronal calcium indicator (far-red sensor) and tp53-dGFP or H2AX-GFP DDR reporters; record 1–2 Hz for network dynamics and bursts at 5–10 Hz during arousal tests.
