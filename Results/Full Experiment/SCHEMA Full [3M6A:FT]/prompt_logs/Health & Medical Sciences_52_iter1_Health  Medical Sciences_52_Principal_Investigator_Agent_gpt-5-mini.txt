  "Risk Factors and Limitations": "DP utility loss: adding DP noise and clipping can reduce explanation fidelity and stability below required thresholds, especially at small ε; results may show sharp utility drops for some explainers. Adversary realism: simulated auxiliary data and stateful attackers may not capture all real-world attacker capabilities (e.g., linked registries, insider access), so measured leakage could underestimate worst-case risk. Sample-size and power: subgroup analyses need large labeled samples per subgroup (thousands) which may be unavailable; small groups will yield wide CIs. Compute and latency: DP-calibrated explainers and red-team suites increase compute cost and may violate low-latency constraints for online high-risk explanations.
