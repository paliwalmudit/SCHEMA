Primary estimator: Callaway & Sant’Anna continuous-dose ATT with exposure lag structures (lags = 0,1,3 years); clustering at country level; covariates include pre-treatment sector shares and trends. Spatial endogeneity: instrument spatial lags with spatial lags of predetermined covariates and neighbors’ predicted allocations; estimate SDM via system-GMM with collapsed instruments and compare to LIML spatial-IV.   "Risk Factors and Limitations": "IV exclusion restriction failures: instruments (predicted-allocation, eligibility, approval timing) may affect outcomes through channels other than funding. Project-approval timing endogeneity: approval lags may correlate with local political shocks. Measurement error: inaccurate geocodes, missing project polygons, or mismeasured capital stocks will attenuate estimates. Data gaps: limited firm-level coverage (ORBIS) and inconsistent national registries reduce validation samples. Weak instruments and instrument proliferation: many instruments can produce weak or biased GMM estimates in finite samples. Interference beyond modeled bands: long-range or sectoral spillovers may violate partial-interference assumptions. Cross-border treatment: non-EU neighbors and asymmetric border effects complicate W matrix design. Multiple testing: many heterogeneous subgroup tests inflate false discovery risk. Computational scale: geospatial joins, bootstraps, and system-GMM are compute intensive and may require substantial resources. External validity: results may not generalize beyond 2000–2019 or to later program designs."
