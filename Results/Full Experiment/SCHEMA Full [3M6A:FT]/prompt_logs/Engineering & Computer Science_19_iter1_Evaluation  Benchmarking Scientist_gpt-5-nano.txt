- Jackknife+: Under exchangeability, jackknife+ intervals satisfy P(Ynew ∈ CI_JP) ≥ 1−α. Failure modes: severe learner instability or degenerate leave-one-out predictors.
- CV+: With K-folds formed exchangeably, CV+ yields marginal coverage ≥1−α; conservatism increases with instability/smaller K.
- Stratified/Mondrian: If exchangeability holds within each stratum, applying split conformal per stratum gives coverage ≥1−α for test points whose stratum distribution matches calibration; failure occurs for tiny/mis-specified strata or covariate shift.
- Weighted conformal: If importance weights consistently estimate p_test/p_train and are bounded, weighted conformal gives asymptotic 1−α coverage; finite-sample validity requires bounded estimation error.
- OOB-with-correction: Naive OOB lacks finite-sample guarantees; provable bounds likely require stability/U-statistic/coupling assumptions (research).

2) Pseudocode (high-level) and complexity
- Jackknife+/CV+ (ensemble-friendly):
  1. Choose K (default 5–10; if n small use K=n for jackknife). Partition data into K folds.
  2. Parallel for each fold k: train model M_{−k} on data\fold_k and produce predictions on fold_k and on test X.
  3. Collect residuals r_{i,k}=|y_i−M_{−k}(x_i)| for i in fold_k and predictions p_{k}(x) for test x.
  4. For each test x form candidate intervals using quantiles of {r_{i,k}+/- p_k(x) shifts} and aggregate per JP/CV+ rule.
  Complexity: time ≈ K·T_train; memory stores K models (may stream if not enough RAM). Parallelize across folds with joblib/process pool.
- OOB correction workflow:
  1. Train single RF with T trees; record in-bag indices per tree.
  2. For each i compute OOB prediction by averaging trees where i was OOB; compute OOB residuals.
  3. Compute empirical OOB quantile q_OOB; optionally inflate q_OOB by stability-derived factor.
  Complexity: single fit + O(T·n) bookkeeping; parallelize per-tree and per-sample aggregation.
