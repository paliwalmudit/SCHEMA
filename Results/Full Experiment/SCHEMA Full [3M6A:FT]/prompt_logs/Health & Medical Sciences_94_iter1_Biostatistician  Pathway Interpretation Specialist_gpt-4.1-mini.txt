Adapter/read structure and sequencing configuration
- Recommended read design: Read1: 8–12 nt inline UMI then 20 nt guide sequence (in-line read of guide); Index1/Index2: unique dual 8 nt indexes. This allows Read1 to capture UMI+sgRNA; Read2 not required for guide mapping but can be used for spike-in or transcriptional barcodes.
