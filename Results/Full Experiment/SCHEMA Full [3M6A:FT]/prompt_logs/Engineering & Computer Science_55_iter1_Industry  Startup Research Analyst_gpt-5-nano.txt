We will treat proposed thresholds as evidence‑informed starting points and report sensitivity ranges. 2) Formal operational formulas
- deviation_score: compute per interaction as 1 − cosine(Emb(instr), Emb(output)) where Emb = normalized sentence embedding (e.g., SBERT) or normalized BLEU/ROUGE for structured tasks; outputs scaled 0–1. - goal_persistence: for a goal_id g over window W (default W = 24 hours or N = 10 successive actions), persistence = (# actions labeled as g within W) / max_possible_actions_in_W. Also compute session persistence across cross_session_state_id with decay weighting (exp(−λΔt), λ tuned to domain).

We will run systematic perturbations: additive token noise (5/10/20% token dropout), paraphrase (back‑translation, synonym substitution at 10/20/30% substitution rates), adversarial edits (targeted prompt injections), OOD context shifts, and semantic distractors (irrelevant extra context). Evaluation: randomized test sets (≥100 tasks per condition), report robustness curves (task_success vs perturbation magnitude), and delta metrics with 95% CIs. Include red‑team adversarial suites (see below).

4) Missing data, imputation, hybrid autonomy labeling
- Required fields: timestamp, deployment_id, model_version, autonomy_level (nullable but flagged), action_type, escalation_flag. Records missing required fields are quarantined.
