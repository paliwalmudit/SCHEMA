Main contributions are: (1) linking unbound tissue exposure to mTORC2-specific PD modulation, (2) testing causality with mechanistic perturbations (AAV-shRictor and AKT S473 mutants), and (3) delivering quantitative histology and exposure–response models. Expected outcome is a reproducible, exposure‑anchored assessment of isosativan’s ability to restore AKT signaling and mitigate diabetic muscle atrophy, informing translational potential and go/no-go decisions for further preclinical development.",

Multiplicity control by false discovery rate.   "Risk Factors and Limitations": "Technical and biological risks:\n- Microdialysis failure: probe placement, low recovery, inflammation around probe may bias Kp,uu; fallback (tissue homogenate binding + PBPK) has higher uncertainty.\n- Assay variability: cross-lab differences for p-AKT assays and LC-MS/MS can cause inconsistent PD readouts. \"p-AKT Ser473\" is a phosphorylated protein measurement;
