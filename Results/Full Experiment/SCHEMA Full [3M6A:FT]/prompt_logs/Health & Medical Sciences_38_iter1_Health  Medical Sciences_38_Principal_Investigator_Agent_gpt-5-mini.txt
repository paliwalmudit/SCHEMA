  "Abstract": "We address the gap between probabilistic diagnosis models and clinically usable explanations by combining an abductive argumentation scheme with Bayesian network probabilities to produce structured, statistical explanations for medical diagnosis predictions. The method instantiates argument templates (claim, premises, warrant, counter-evidence) where each premise is scored by conditional probabilities and converted into likelihood evidence that supports or challenges candidate diagnoses. We add causal-DAG constraints to forbid medically impossible edits, a plausibility classifier trained on joint EHR distributions to filter counterfactuals, and Bayesian Monte Carlo propagation to report posterior probability intervals.
