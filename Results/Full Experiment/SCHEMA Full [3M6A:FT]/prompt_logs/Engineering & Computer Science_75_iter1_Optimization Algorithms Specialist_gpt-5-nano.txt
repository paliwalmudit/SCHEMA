 Taxonomy of DAO heuristics and algorithmic considerations

  - Strengths: fast per-iteration, transparent decisions; scalable with careful pruning.
  - Limitations: prone to local optima; limited global trade-off exploration.

- Local search and continuous weight refinement
  - Core idea: fix aperture topology while optimising continuous leaf weights and jaw positions.
  - Strengths: excellent plan quality with fewer apertures; good integration with leaf sequencing.
  - Limitations: still combinatorially heavy if topology changes are frequent.

- Simulated annealing and tabu search
  - Core idea: explore beyond local optima using probabilistic moves (SA) or memory-based prohibitions (TS).
  - Strengths: robust to local traps; adaptable to complex constraints.
  - Limitations: runtime can be long; performance sensitive to cooling schedules and tabu horizons.

- Genetic algorithms and evolutionary strategies
  - Core idea: populations of aperture sets evolve via crossover and mutation; fitness reflects dosimetric and deliverability criteria.
  - Strengths: global search capability; flexible encoding of discrete apertures.
  - Limitations: computationally intensive; design of representation and operators is critical.

- Column generation and hybrid master/pricing formulations
  - Core idea: generate promising apertures on demand, solved via a master problem with a pricing subproblem.
  - Strengths: scalable handling of large aperture pools; strong theoretical grounding.
  - Limitations: implementation complexity; depends on effective pricing subproblem.

- Mixed-integer programming relaxations and exact methods
  - Core idea: discretise apertures and formulate DAO as MIP with delivery constraints.
  - Strengths: provable bounds; reproducibility with transparent formulations.
  - Limitations: limited to modest problem sizes unless advanced cuts and decomposition are used.

- Hybrid machine learning and surrogate-guided approaches
  - Core idea: use ML to predict promising apertures or to warm-start optimization; can include reinforcement learning.
  - Strengths: substantial speedups; potential for patient-specific adaptation.

  - Core idea: fix aperture topology while optimising continuous leaf weights and jaw positions.
  - Strengths: excellent plan quality with fewer apertures; good integration with leaf sequencing.
  - Limitations: still combinatorially heavy if topology changes are frequent.

- Simulated annealing and tabu search
  - Core idea: explore beyond local optima using probabilistic moves (SA) or memory-based prohibitions (TS).
  - Strengths: robust to local traps; adaptable to complex constraints.
  - Limitations: runtime can be long; performance sensitive to cooling schedules and tabu horizons.

- Genetic algorithms and evolutionary strategies
  - Core idea: populations of aperture sets evolve via crossover and mutation; fitness reflects dosimetric and deliverability criteria.
  - Strengths: global search capability; flexible encoding of discrete apertures.
  - Limitations: computationally intensive; design of representation and operators is critical.

- Column generation and hybrid master/pricing formulations
  - Core idea: generate promising apertures on demand, solved via a master problem with a pricing subproblem.
  - Strengths: scalable handling of large aperture pools; strong theoretical grounding.
  - Limitations: implementation complexity; depends on effective pricing subproblem.

  - Core idea: discretise apertures and formulate DAO as MIP with delivery constraints.
  - Strengths: provable bounds; reproducibility with transparent formulations.

- Provide a compact submission package including anonymized DICOM-RT, aperture exports, deterministic container tag, seed file, CI logs, and a failure-mode reporting template with fields for reproduction steps and severity.
