  "Risk Factors and Limitations": "Toxicity and regulation: mercury handling limits sample throughput and requires strict waste manifests and licensed disposal that can delay experiments. Sample stability: ligand exchange, oxidation, or humidity may degrade clusters and narrow transparency windows. Ligand vibrational overlap: bulky ligands can still produce mid-IR bands that raise k(ω) above 0.01 in key ranges. Alignment scalability: achieving macroscopic alignment of discrete clusters in defect-free films may be limited by aggregation, domain size, or polymer-matrix compatibility. Scattering and morphology: surface roughness or particulate inclusions increase apparent k(ω) via scattering and complicate Mie corrections. Structural resolution: inability to obtain suitable single crystals forces reliance on microED or PDF which have interpretation limits. Computational limits: GW/BSE with SOC and converged phonons is computationally expensive and may be infeasible for large ligand-decorated clusters; codes may not support full DFPT+SOC and finite-displacement approximations introduce errors. Generalization: results for Hg-based clusters may not directly transfer to other chemistries due to strong relativistic effects unique to Hg."
