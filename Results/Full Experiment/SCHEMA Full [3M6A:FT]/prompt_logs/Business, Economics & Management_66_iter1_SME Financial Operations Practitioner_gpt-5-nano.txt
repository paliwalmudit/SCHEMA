- 3–5 scored examples for validation: (a) Case A yields 87 (audit-ready); (b) Case B yields 43 (requires remediation); (c) Case C after training yields 93 (ready for external audit).
- Inter-rater reliability plan: pre-validation double coding on 50 transactions; target ICC (two-way random) ≥ 0.75 for the composite; Cohen’s kappa ≥ 0.60 for binary items. If targets aren’t met, perform targeted retraining and repeat validation.

Power and sample size
- Baseline assumption: use variance from pilot baseline; when unavailable, assume ICC in the 0.02–0.05 range as a planning bound.
- Primary outcome sensitivity: detect a 10–15 percentage-point change in error rate with 80% power and alpha 0.05, adjusted for clustering via design effect.
- Practical target: for a single UMKM over 6–12 months with ~300 transactions/month, plan to collect 1,800–3,600 observations post-intervention; exact numbers to be finalized after baseline variance is estimated by the Impact Evaluation lead.

Donor-pool and synthetic control specifications
- Exact matching covariates: region, business size (revenue/transactions), baseline error rate, bank access, owner education, seasonality, and prior use of formal accounting.
- Inclusion criteria: similar micro/SME profile, available baseline data for at least 6 months, willingness to share de-identified data.
- Exclusion criteria: major deviations in operation type or regulatory status.
- Pre-trend checks: visual inspection of pre-intervention trajectories; RMSPE threshold target ≤ 5% of pre-intervention outcome scale.
- Documentation: a donor-pool eligibility log and pre-trend diagnostic report templates.

Materiality and aggregation rules
- Materiality thresholds: absolute mismatch per transaction ≥ IDR 50,000; relative mismatch ≥ 1% of monthly receipts.
- Aggregation: report monthly aggregated mismatches when total monthly mismatch ≥ 1% of receipts or when more than 5% of transactions show mismatch; provide a summarized exception note.

Verifier QA and training
- Training syllabus outline: verifier concepts, sampling, chain-of-custody, document review, and adjudication procedures.
- Double-coding: start at 20% of transactions; drop to 10% after achieving ICC ≥ 0.75.
- Adjudication SOP: third-party lead reviewer reconciles persistent disagreements; retraining triggered if ICC < 0.75 or adjudication disagreement > 15% across two consecutive months.

Pre-registration and transparency
- Registry: OSF or AEA registry; pre-specify primary/secondary outcomes, model specs, stopping rules.
- Data/code sharing: commit to publishing code and de-identified or synthetic data where permissible; provide a timeline for releases.
- Timeline: pre-registration and protocol finalization within 2–3 weeks; code and templates released in Week 4–6.

Operational COA and journal samples

- Offline capture: Google Forms with offline capability; local device storage; weekly sync; versioning and audit trail in the central ledger.

Budget and ethics

Fidelity and bias
- Adherence metrics: ≥80% of transactions captured; ≥90% complete documentation; retraining triggers at two consecutive months of <70% adherence.
- Hawthorne bias mitigation: rotate auditors when possible, emphasize routine operations, and include placebo outcomes for sensitivity analyses.
