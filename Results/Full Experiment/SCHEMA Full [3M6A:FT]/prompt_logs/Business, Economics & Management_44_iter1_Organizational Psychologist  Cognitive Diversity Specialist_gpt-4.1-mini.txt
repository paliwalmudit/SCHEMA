Archival proxies such as prior-year inspections, restatements, formal sanctions, and large audit adjustments (top decile) will be pre-specified to augment sparse inspection data as secondary outcomes.

For missing data and attrition, we will implement Multiple Imputation by Chained Equations (MICE) with 50 imputations incorporating a rich auxiliary set including baseline team demographics, past audit quality indicators, firm governance metrics, client complexity, and treatment assignment indicators. Sensitivity analyses using Lee bounds, pattern-mixture MNAR δ-shift models, and Oster bounds for omitted-variable bias will be pre-registered. Operational triggers will be set to increase sample size or intensify follow-up if attrition exceeds 15% overall or 20% by treatment arm.

Compliance will be operationalized as completing at least 80% of assigned treatment elements within prescribed windows, verified via attendance logs with timestamps, LMS/module completion records, signed checklists, and facilitator notes. We will pre-specify balance, monotonicity, and McCrary density tests for manipulation and first-stage strength diagnostics; if IV assumptions weaken, partial-identification bounds including Angrist-Imbens and Lee bounds will be reported.

Blinded archival coding protocols will mandate dual independent coding of audit adjustments, restatements, and opinion modifications with coders blind to treatment and firm identity. Disagreements exceeding Cohen’s kappa 0.70 thresholds will trigger adjudication by a senior coder, retraining, and re-coding of random 20% samples to maintain inter-rater reliability.

Measurement precision for discretionary accruals will follow the Modified-Jones model using a standard three-year estimation window, reporting alternatives (Jones, Kothari) in sensitivity tests. Audit adjustments will be summed and normalized by prior-year assets, while audit delay will be measured as days from fiscal year-end to audit report date, with both raw and log-transformed values analyzed.

Finally, mediation claims will be cautiously framed; causal language reserved for randomized mediators only. Observational mediation analyses will incorporate sequential ignorability sensitivity checks, Imai bootstrapped confidence intervals, Oster bounds, and present indirect effect estimates as ranges or bounds for rigorous interpretation.

These operationalizations and psychometric plans will be finalized in time to meet the two-week deadline, enabling prompt pilot deployment. I remain committed to close collaboration with econometrics, regulatory, and field teams to integrate measurement with analysis and field realities, ensuring this project’s methodological rigor and contextual appropriateness advance our understanding of cognitive diversity’s impact on audit quality in Vietnam.
