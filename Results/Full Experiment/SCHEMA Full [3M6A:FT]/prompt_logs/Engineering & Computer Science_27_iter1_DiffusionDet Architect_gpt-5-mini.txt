- Baseline single model (S=10 steps): ~200 GFLOPs/image, ~80 ms/image on A100.  
- Ensemble M=4: ~800 GFLOPs, ~320 ms/image (linear scaling).  
- MC dropout N_mc=16: ~3.2k GFLOPs if done naively, ~640 ms/image (multiplicative).  
- Single-model uncertainty head (predict mean+variance per-box): +~5% FLOPs (~10 GFLOPs), +≈5–10 ms latency; last-layer Laplace approximation similar cost with lower latency.  
- Production recommendation: single-model uncertainty head + temperature scaling for low-latency deployment; reserve ensembles/MC for offline calibration.

8) Projection + Gaussian splatting pseudocode (numeric)
- For each LiDAR point p: project u,v = project(p,R,t,fx,fy,cx,cy); compute feature f_p = MLP(point_features) d=64; splat to feature map F at floor(u/s),floor(v/s) with weight w=exp(−((u−up)^2+(v−vp)^2)/(2σ^2)), σ=1.5 px (feature scale); accumulate F += w·f_p and W += w; final F = F / (W+1e−6). Use depth bins=8 channels + density + height_max → total BEV channels ~11.
