  "Risk Factors and Limitations": "Technical failure modes: DP causes reduced model utility at low ε (large noise); MPC (MP-SPDZ) introduces substantial latency and CPU/bandwidth overhead in WAN settings; ZK proving time can be tens of seconds for complex circuits, raising latency and cost. Security/modeling limits: threat model variations (semi-honest vs malicious parties) change guarantees; protocol thresholds (t) may not match real attacker budgets. Data limitations: fraud is rare and non-stationary—synthetic data may not fully capture real attacker behavior; label noise and dispute delays degrade supervised learning. Evaluation biases: temporally stratified splits may still leak correlated patterns; metrics like AUPRC are sensitive to class prevalence and require careful sampling for comparison. Scalability and cost: end-to-end system (DP+MPC+ZK+blockchain anchoring) increases compute, network, and storage costs compared with ML-only solutions and may not be affordable at very large transaction volumes. Generalization: tuned pilot hardware and chosen instance types may not reflect smaller edge devices; results may not transfer to different regulatory or cross-jurisdictional data sharing constraints. Regulatory limits: on-chain anchoring of metadata must avoid exposure of PII or regulatory-prohibited data flows; legal constraints can limit deployment scenarios."
