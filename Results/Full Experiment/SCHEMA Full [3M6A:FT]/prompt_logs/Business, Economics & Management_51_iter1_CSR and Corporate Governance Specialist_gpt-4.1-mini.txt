- CSR depth: multidimensional engagement capturing strategy integration, governance presence (ESG committees, board diversity), resource allocation, and stakeholder dialogue; triangulate with content analysis of sustainability reports and surveys.
- Eco-innovation: product and process eco-innovation indicators (green patents, green product share of revenue, energy/woss reductions, ISO 14001/EMAS adoption, green procurement practices).
- TMS and governance: environmental leadership measures, executive incentives aligned with sustainability, board composition, ESG committee existence, and governance quality proxies from annual reports and surveys.
- Sustainability performance: environmental KPIs (emissions, energy intensity, water use, waste), social outcomes, and third-party ESG scores.

Data strategy
- Build firm-year panel data across manufacturing, utilities, and services in 2–4 countries, merging: firm financials; green-finance records; ESG/rating data; environmental KPIs; patent/innovation indicators; governance data; and survey-based TMS/CSR measures.
- Supplement with 8–12 case-study firms for process-tracing of green-finance-triggered changes.

Identification strategies
- Baseline fixed effects to control unobserved time-invariant heterogeneity; include time fixed effects and macro controls.
- Difference-in-differences exploiting policy shocks or country-level green-finance incentives; use staggered adoption designs where possible.
- Instrumental variables for green finance access: policy environment indices, bank-level green-lending propensity, or regulatory incentives; apply two-stage least squares with strong relevance tests.
- Dynamic panels: system GMM to address persistence and potential reverse causality.

Modeling plan for mediation and moderation
- Use SEM to test sequential mediation (CSR depth and eco-innovation as mediators) and multilevel SEM to accommodate country/industry nesting.
- Test moderation with interactions: TMS × green finance (strengthening CSR/adoption) and CSR × governance quality (barriers/remedies to absorption).
- Where sample size is limited, treat product/process eco-innovation as separate mediators within a path-analysis framework or as latent constructs in SEM.
