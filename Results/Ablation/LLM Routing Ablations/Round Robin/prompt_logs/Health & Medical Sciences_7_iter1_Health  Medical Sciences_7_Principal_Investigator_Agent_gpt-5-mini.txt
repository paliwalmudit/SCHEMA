  "Risk Factors and Limitations": "1) Data and labeling biases: multi-center labels may vary; STAPLE adjudication may not remove all systematic label errors. 2) Lack of measured sinograms for many centers: simulated sinograms may not capture true device physics, producing domain gaps. 3) Privacy trade-offs: DP applied to clinical embeddings can degrade downstream utility; acceptable ε thresholds are uncertain. 4) Computational cost and scalability: full-scale diffusion pretraining requires large GPU clusters and long wall-clock time; fallback schedules may underperform. 5) Failure modes in physics loss: incorrect forward-model parameters can harm rather than help HU fidelity. 6) Generalization limits: model may not generalize to rare scanner models, extreme dose regimes, or populations underrepresented in training. 7) Evaluation bias: held-out sets may still share latent similarities with training centers, inflating apparent performance. 8) Regulatory risk: intended-use claims limited to assistive support; clinical adoption depends on prospective trials beyond retrospective metrics. 9) Synthetic-data risks: generative artifacts may misrepresent small lesions or subtle attenuation changes, leading to downstream clinical errors. 10) Uncertainty quantification limits: ensemble methods increase compute and may still fail on out-of-distribution cases."
