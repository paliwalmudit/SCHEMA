  "Abstract": "Respiratory motion degrades cardiac cine MRI, reducing contour accuracy and clinical confidence. We propose a unified approach that combines a controlled-motion cardiac cine dataset, physics-informed motion augmentation, and temporal, motion-aware deep networks to improve robustness of myocardial segmentation and automated image-quality assessment under respiratory artifacts. The method augments training data with realistic, learnable displacement fields derived from respiratory traces and parameterized motion models, trains a hybrid 2D+time network with explicit short-frame motion blocks and respiratory-phase conditioning, and produces calibrated per-pixel probability maps for segmentation plus a motion-severity classifier. Main contributions are: (1) a reproducible motion-augmentation pipeline that preserves anatomy, (2) a temporal architecture that leverages short-frame displacement estimates, and (3) integrated uncertainty calibration and multi-task evaluation on vendor holdout folds. We expect improved segmentation DSC on held-out vendors, better preservation of EDV/ESV/EF accuracy under severe motion, and calibrated uncertainty maps that flag unreliable frames for review.",
