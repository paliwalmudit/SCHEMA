Subgroup minima: farmers n≥150, advisors n≥100, other stakeholder groups ≥50 each. Power rule-of-thumb: with n≈100 per group you can detect standardized mean differences ≈0.4 (moderate) with 80% power; to detect small effects (d≈0.25–0.30) plan n≥250 per comparison group or pool via weighting.

BWS design and analysis choices (locked)
