1) Labeling sample-size & oversampling strategy
- Pilot: 100 stratified labels (2 weeks) to estimate prevalence per project/language.

- Use binomial CI: for ±5% margin at 95% CI, n≈385 (worst-case). Thus require ≈385 positive-labelled spans to estimate recall/precision with ±5%.
