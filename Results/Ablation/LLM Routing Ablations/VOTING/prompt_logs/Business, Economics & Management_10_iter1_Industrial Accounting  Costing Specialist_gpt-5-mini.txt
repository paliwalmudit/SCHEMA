To make modularity effects measurable and auditable, research should focus on three complementary tracks: (1) accounting model redesign, (2) pragmatic proxies and validation methods, and (3) operational data-governance and KPIs.

Accounting model redesign: define a module-level costing template that becomes the canonical analytic unit. Each module-level cost statement should include direct material (supplier invoice-linked), direct labor (routings and time tickets), manufacturing overhead allocated via activity-based or time-driven ABC drivers (machine-hours, setup counts, test cycles), engineering and NRE amortization (capitalized design apportioned by forecasted unit volumes or expected module lifetime), assembly/test, logistics, warranty/service provisions, inventory carrying and obsolescence allocations, and supplier-specific landed-cost detail.
