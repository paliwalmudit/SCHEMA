Remarks and proof sketch. The sufficiency follows from standard universal approximation + policy optimization: for each fixed w treat as single-objective RL. Necessity: continuity imposes that the image of Δ under a continuous map is connected; thus disconnected Pareto sets require either multi-headed/multimodal output or discontinuous mappings.

3) Role and benefit of multi-headed actors (informal proposition)
Proposition B (Multi-head capacity). A multi-headed actor with H heads and a head-selection/interpolation mechanism increases representational capacity for multimodal and disconnected solution sets. Implication. Multi-headed architectures act like a mixture-of-experts and mitigate preference-to-action non-injectivity; they are especially useful when scalarized optimization yields multiple local optima.
