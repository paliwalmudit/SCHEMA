To support the finalized stratified purposive sampling frame of 12 schools spanning urban/rural and primary/secondary strata, I will initiate timely collaboration with school administrators and lead teachers to secure access and schedule the minimum of three classroom recording sessions per stratum. These sessions will purposively cover designated subject areas (English, Maths, Science) and activity types to support rich variation in repair events. I will ensure that scheduling is minimally disruptive by aligning with existing timetables and securing teacher buy-in through transparent communication of study aims and procedures.

Consent and ethical protocols remain a top priority. I will implement culturally appropriate informed consent and assent procedures, using clear, jargon-free explanations of repair initiation research, emphasizing voluntary participation, confidentiality, and the right to withdraw. Parental consent processes will be closely adhered to for minors, with careful documentation of retention and deletion schedules in line with IRB approvals and Ghanaian ethical standards.

During recordings, I will conduct systematic onsite monitoring and troubleshooting—optimizing microphone placement, audiovisual synchronization, and shielding from background noise—to maximize capture fidelity of verbal, prosodic, gaze, and gesture data critical for Jeffersonian multimodal transcription and CA analysis. I will also maintain detailed field notes and metadata including classroom layout, seating arrangements, participant demographics, language policy, lesson stages, and observed interactional norms to facilitate cross-case contextualization.

To embed Ghanaian sociolinguistic and cultural validity, I will coordinate with local language-fluent informants and transcribers during calibration phases for collaborative development and maintenance of the living glossary of repair tokens and code-switch markers. I will facilitate their participation in calibration sessions and adjudication discussions to ensure nuanced comprehension of local repair phenomena and embodied cues.

Data security and participant privacy will be rigorously enforced by ensuring all raw identifiable media remain encrypted with tightly controlled access. I will oversee secure, timely data transfer to the Data Manager and Transcription team, coordinating anonymization pilots with careful validation that prosodic and gesture information is preserved post face-blurring and voice-minimizing transformations. Documentation of these procedures will be clear and transparent.

To monitor and mitigate observer effects and support habituation, I will track participation stability, changes in classroom interaction patterns over at least three sessions per class, and maintain comparative field note analyses of early versus later sessions to assess naturalistic interaction emergence.

Finally, I will maintain open and consistent communication channels between schools, transcription teams, and analysis leads, scheduling calibration meetings and transcription workshops, and promptly reporting operational challenges or participant feedback. I remain fully dedicated to ensuring our data collection is ethically sound, methodologically robust, culturally responsive, and executed smoothly to support the analytic rigor and high-quality outputs of this important CA study on repair initiation in Ghanaian ESL classrooms.
