- Sigmoid σ(·): E[σ(x)] ≈ σ(μ) + 0.5 σ''(μ) σ_x^2, Var[σ(x)] ≈ σ'(μ)^2 σ_x^2. For correlated inputs, use Jacobian and diagonal+low-rank Σ.
- Softmax mapping from logits z to probabilities p: use delta-method in logit space.
  - Mean: E[p] ≈ softmax(μ_z) + 0.5 vec(Tr(H_k Σ_z)) across classes k, where H_k is Hessian of p_k at μ_z. In practice compute mean ≈ softmax(μ_z) and include second-order correction only when significant.
  - Covariance: Cov[p] ≈ J(μ_z) Σ_z J(μ_z)^T with J_ij = p_i (δ_ij - p_j). Compute efficiently using low-rank Σ_z or Hutchinson trace approximations.

Practical cost/accuracy trade-offs:
- Order-1 (Jacobian-only) gives covariance cheaply: Cov ≈ J Σ J^T. Add second-order mean correction (1/2 Tr(H Σ)) only where necessary: bottleneck layers, attention modules, or early layers with large Σ.
- Use low-rank+diag or per-channel diagonal Σ for decoder full-res layers; keep higher-fidelity Σ at coarse bottleneck. Kronecker factorization (spatial × channel) is useful mid-budget.
- Compute Tr(H Σ) via Tr(Σ H) using low-rank factors or Hutchinson sampling (few probes) to avoid full Hessian.

Theoretical remainder and diagnostics:
- Second-order Taylor remainder for vector f: ||R|| ≤ (1/6) sup_{ξ} ||D^3 f(ξ)|| · E[||x-μ||^3]. For Gaussian x, use E[||x-μ||^3] ≤ (trace Σ)^{3/2}. Thus approximate bound: ||R|| ≲ (1/6) M_3 (trace Σ)^{3/2}, where M_3 = sup norm of third derivatives (can be bounded for activations).

Stability & regularization:
- Always enforce Σ = L L^T + diag(d) with d ≥ ε and add jitter εI. Clip eigenvalues or penalize large trace growth per-layer. Constrain spectral norm of conv kernels to limit covariance amplification.
