Key design ideas to fill gaps
- Latent attention with uncertainty: Represent attention as a distribution over the N instances in a bag. A tractable choice is a Dirichlet-distributed vector a ∼ Dirichlet(α(x)) so that the attention weights sum to one and reflect uncertainty. The network predicts α(x) to shape the prior/posterior over a. For flexible optimization, use a reparameterizable surrogate (e.g., Gamma-based stick-breaking or a Relaxed-Dirichlet) to enable gradient-based VI.
- Local and global coupling: Incorporate local smoothing by conditioning each a_i on the features of its neighborhood (N_i) via a small graph or edge-conditioned network. Introduce a global latent variable z_g capturing slide/scan-wide context with a prior p(z_g) and posterior q(z_g|x). The per-instance attention a_i depends on x_i, local neighborhood info, and z_g, enabling both local coherence and global adaptation.
- Objective and inference: Use variational inference with an ELBO objective that jointly optimizes the bag label likelihood and the KL divergences:
  ELBO = E_{q(a|x)q(z_g|x)}[ log p(y | a, z_g, x) ] − KL(q(a|x) || p(a)) − KL(q(z_g|x) || p(z_g)).
  The bag label probability can be produced by a differentiable weighted pooling of instance features, e.g., y_hat = f_pool( {a_i x_i} ), followed by a classifier. Optional auxiliary tasks (reconstructing x from latent variables) can stabilize learning.
