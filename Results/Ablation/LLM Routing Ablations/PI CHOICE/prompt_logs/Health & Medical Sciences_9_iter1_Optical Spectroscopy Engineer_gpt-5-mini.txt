Use two‑point per‑run normalization plus periodic full calibration against an extracted‑pigment standard in agar. Provide two non‑biological controls on each lot: a printed spectral dye patch matched to granadaene ±5 nm and an extracted/inactivated pigment matrix validated non‑viable. Per‑lot acceptance: printed patch OD within ±10%, QC dye absorbance drift ≤±0.05 AU, LOQ CV ≤20%.

Calibration transfer and retraining: use Bayesian hierarchical or mixed‑effects calibration with lot/instrument random effects. Minimal per‑lot retraining dataset ~30 runs spanning blanks and three concentration standards across ≥3 instruments to update priors and transfer calibration.

Biosafety and sealed‑cartridge design: sealed incubation chamber with one‑way valves, post‑run activation of an internal bactericidal reservoir (e.g., bleach or validated denaturant), mechanical lock after run, integrated absorbent pad and single‑use disposal. Do not include live positive controls on‑cartridge. Site disposal per biohazard policy (autoclave/incineration) with operator PPE (nitrile gloves, eye protection) and a short training script.

Test procedures and acceptance: (1) spectrometer scan 350–800 nm for peak selection; (2) component bench tests for LED/filter/detector responsivity and stray light; (3) cartridge‑level blank and printed standard reads; (4) biological calibration with serial CFU plating/time‑course to derive LOD/LOQ and ROC thresholds. Report A = -log10(Isample/Iref) converted to CFU‑equivalent with 95% prediction interval, QC flags, and action bands (Negative <10^2 CFU‑eq; Gray 10^2–10^4 CFU‑eq → reflex NAAT; Positive ≥10^4 CFU‑eq → recommend prophylaxis).
