  "Risk Factors and Limitations": "Monetization aggregation risk: combining heterogeneous impacts into a single euro surcharge can double-count or obscure drivers of damage; different biodiversity valuation methods yield divergent surcharges. Licensing limits: key LCA inventories (e.g., ecoinvent) are license-restricted and cannot be redistributed, limiting direct reproducibility of raw inputs. Weak instrument risk: the 15% binding draw may produce low compliance or weak first-stage relationships (F<10), reducing precision of 2SLS LATE estimates. Attention and measurement error: mediators measured post-treatment may be noisy or influenced by measurement itself. External validity: online survey tasks and a selected SKU may not generalize to in-store behavior or other product categories. Dependence across tasks: if respondents face multiple choice tasks, within-respondent correlation can reduce effective sample size and bias naive SEs. Legal and regulatory constraints: country-specific price-display and competition rules may restrict label wording or implementation. Sample bias: panel recruitment and quotas may not fully match the broader shopper population, affecting transferability. Computational limits: detailed monetization pipelines with spatialized indicators can be compute-intensive and license-dependent, limiting rapid re-running for many SKUs."
