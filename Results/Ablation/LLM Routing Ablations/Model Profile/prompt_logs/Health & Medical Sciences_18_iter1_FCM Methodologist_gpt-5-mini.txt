I will compute DoA and downstream metrics under three within-subject normalizations: (a) max-absolute scaling, (b) rank-based scaling (preserves ordinal importance), and (c) robust z-score (median/MAD). For each method we will report DoA distributions, test–retest ICC(2,1), clustering outcomes, and sensitivity of primary inferences; the final pre-registered primary normalization will be chosen based on pilot evidence but all alternatives will be retained and published.

3) Canonicalization and promotion rules
Canonicalization pipeline: lowercase/strip punctuation → exact dictionary match → Levenshtein fuzzy match (threshold 0.85) → semantic-embedding NN (cosine ≥0.60) → otherwise mark novel. Promote participant-unique nodes to canonical if frequency ≥5% of participants OR present in semantic cluster supported by ≥3 participants, with manual adjudication logged. All mapping decisions will be auditable.

4) Link confidence and rationale integration
Per-link confidence will serve dual roles: used as weights for aggregated consensus (confidence-weighted mean and median) and included as a moderator in hierarchical models (interaction with link weights). Free-text rationales will be processed via an NLP-assisted pipeline (embedding-based clustering and topic modelling to propose codes), followed by manual dual-coder validation to build a reproducible codebook; codes will be included as categorical predictors or moderators.

5) Imputation and uncertainty propagation
Default: absent links treated as zero. Sensitivity analyses will include small-noise imputation and multiple imputation across canonicalization realizations. We will propagate canonicalization and imputation uncertainty by re-running Bayesian hierarchical models across sampled canonicalization variants and pooling posteriors to reflect mapping uncertainty.

6) Hierarchical/Bayesian modeling
Primary models: Bayesian multilevel models with vignettes nested in clinicians nested in sites; for DoA (bounded 0–1) we will fit a logistic (logit) linear model or Beta hierarchical model as appropriate. Weakly informative priors: intercept ~ Normal(0,1) on logit scale, coefficients ~ Normal(0,1), group SDs ~ Half‑Normal(0,1). Models will be checked via posterior predictive checks, R̂, ESS, and compared by LOO‑CV/WAIC.

7) Behavioral validators and UX pilot
We will collect time-to-decision, acceptance of CDSS recommendation, ordering/treatment changes pre/post explanation, and UI dwell time as behavioral validators correlated with DoA. I will run a UX pilot with 5–8 clinicians to validate the 6–8 minute target and produce variance estimates for sample-size simulations.

8) Reproducibility and deliverables

Initial deliverables: DoA prototype and canonicalization scripts within 1 week, normalization comparison and Bayesian model templates within 2 weeks.
