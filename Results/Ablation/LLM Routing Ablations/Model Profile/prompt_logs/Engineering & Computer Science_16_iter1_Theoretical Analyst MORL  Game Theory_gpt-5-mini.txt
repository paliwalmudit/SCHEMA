Theoretical Analyst (MORL & Game Theory): "Summary: below I formalize assumptions, state three concise theorems with proof sketches and explicit constants, characterize the linear-scalarization limitation, quantify how critic error degrades policy improvement, and give concrete empirical verifications and actionable design rules.

Assumptions (precise).
1) Preference domain P = Δ^{m-1} with Euclidean norm. States S and actions A ⊂ R^d with norm ||·||2. Deterministic target policy family π_p : S → A.
2) Policy Lipschitz in p: there exists L_pi ≥ 0 such that for all p,q∈P and s∈S, ||π_p(s)-π_q(s)|| ≤ L_pi ||p-q||.
3) Actor network class F_θ(p,s) is universal for continuous conditional maps and is L_net-Lipschitz in its input p (uniformly in s) after fixing θ-regions; attention/fusion map a(p) is L_att-Lipschitz mapping P→Δ^{K-1}.
4) Critic approximation error bounded: for scalarized preferences p, sup_{s,a}|Q̂_p(s,a)-Q_p(s,a)| ≤ ε_Q.

Theorem 1 (existence/uniform approximation).
If π_p is continuous in p and F_θ is a universal approximator of continuous conditional maps, then for any ε>0 there exists θ such that sup_{p∈P,s∈S} ||F_θ(p,s)-π_p(s)|| ≤ ε.
Proof sketch: continuity of p→π_p and density of conditional network classes (UVFA argument) imply uniform approximation on compact P×S by standard Stone–Weierstrass / universal approx results.

Theorem 2 (K-head discretization bound and fusion improvement).
Let {p_k}_{k=1..K} be points that cover P with radius δ: ∀p ∃k s.t. ||p-p_k|| ≤ δ. Suppose discrete heads a_k(s) satisfy sup_s ||a_k(s)-π_{p_k}(s)|| ≤ ε_head. Then for any p the nearest-head policy error satisfies sup_s ||π_p(s)-a_{k(p)}(s)|| ≤ L_pi·δ + ε_head. If fusion forms action A_fus(p,s)=Σ_k w_k(p) a_k(s) with w(p) Lipschitz L_w and heads as above, then
sup_s ||π_p(s)-A_fus(p,s)|| ≤ ε_head + L_pi·δ + L_w·M·δ,
where M = sup_{p,s}||π_p(s)||+max_k sup_s||a_k(s)|| bounds action scale.
Proof sketch: triangle inequality: π_p ≈ π_{p_k} (≤L_pi δ), π_{p_k} ≈ a_k (≤ε_head). Fusion interpolation introduces additional error from imperfect interpolation weights bounded by L_w·δ times action scale.

Corollaries and design rule: to guarantee ε_total accuracy choose K so cover radius δ ≤ (ε_target - ε_head)/(L_pi + L_w·M). Fusion reduces required K by leveraging continuity via L_w and L_net.

Theorem 3 (linear scalarization convex-hull limitation).
Maximizing expected weighted sum p·E[R] (for fixed p) can only recover Pareto points that are supported by a supporting hyperplane with normal p; equivalently, weighted-sum methods cannot produce Pareto points in strictly non-convex regions of the achievable objective set. Nonlinear scalarizers (e.g., Chebyshev: minimize max_i λ_i|z_i - R_i| with appropriate reference z, or learned non-linear g_φ(p,R)) can produce non-convex Pareto points given sufficient representational power and training regimes that explore multiple basins.
Practical implication: include Chebyshev or learned scalarizers when benchmark objectives show non-convex fronts.

Effect of critic error on policy improvement (quantified).
Under bounded critic error ε_Q, the one-step performance improvement guarantee for deterministic policy gradient degrades additively: J_p(π_new) ≥ J_p(π_old) + α·|∇̂J|^2 - C·ε_Q/(1-γ), where C depends on M and gamma; in short, large ε_Q (relative to actor gradient signal) can reverse improvement. Actionable controls: use twin critics (TD3), small actor update steps, target smoothing, spectral norm or gradient clipping on critics to keep ε_Q small.

Empirical verification recipes.
1) Estimate L_emp ≈ median_{sample pairs p,q} E_s[||π̂_p(s)-π̂_q(s)||/||p-q||] and check L_emp ≤ L_pi_target.
2) Measure head approximation errors ε_head by training single-objective (or p_k) policies and computing sup_s||a_k(s)-π_{p_k}(s)|| on held-out states.
3) Monitor sup TD residual ≈ max batch |r_p+γ Q̂(next)-Q̂(current)| to bound ε_Q trend.
4) Test scalarizer coverage by comparing learned front to oracle (weighted single-objective runs) and running Chebyshev ablation.

Immediate actionable recommendations.
Use fusion by default, regularize network Lipschitz (spectral norm), pick K so δ ≤ ε/(2L_pi) as initial heuristic, maintain twin critics and small actor-step sizes, and include Chebyshev/learned scalarizer ablations. These steps yield explicit, testable bounds linking K, L_pi, ε_head, L_w, and critic error to approximation and improvement guarantees."
