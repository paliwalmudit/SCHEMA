1) Candidate items (horizontal vs vertical) for Ghana, Kenya, Senegal: horizontal (inclusive identity) examples — “How strongly do you identify with people from a different ethnic group than your own?” (4‑point Likert), “In the past 12 months, how often have you socialized with friends from a different religion or ethnic group?” (Never/Once/Several times/Often), and a 3‑name friend roster capturing each friend’s group; vertical (inclusive identity) examples — “To what extent do you feel the national government treats people like you as full members of the nation?” (4‑point Likert) and “People from other groups have equal access to political office” (Agree/Disagree). These will be cognitively tested and localized.

2) Translation/decentering protocol (stepwise): two independent forward translations; reconciliation by local adjudication committee including cultural experts; independent back‑translation; committee adjudication with documented translation checklist; cognitive interviews (30–50 per major language) using think‑aloud probes; iterative revision and final harmonized wording with translation notes.
