- Two tractable linearizations (implementation recipes):
  a) Binary budget (exact for binary full‑loss decisions). Introduce binary variables w_j ∈ {0,1} indicating whether facility j suffers its full degradation ū_j. Then enforce for every j:
    Σ_i x_ij ≤ s_j y_j − ū_j w_j,
    Σ_j w_j ≤ Γ,
    w_j ≤ y_j.
    This is an MILP and exactly models the case "facility j either loses ū_j capacity or not", adversary chooses up to Γ facilities.
  b) Continuous (Bertsimas–Sim style) relaxation (tractable approximation). Allow 0≤w_j≤y_j (continuous) and Σ_j w_j ≤ Γ. Enforce:
    Σ_i x_ij ≤ s_j y_j − ū_j w_j  for all j,

  Σ_{i∈C} x_ij ≤ s_j y_j − α, where α = Σ_{i∈C} d_i − s_j > 0.
