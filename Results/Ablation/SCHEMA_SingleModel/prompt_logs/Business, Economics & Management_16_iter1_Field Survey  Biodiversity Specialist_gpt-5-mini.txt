Cropland—Rainfed: strong seasonal NDVI and fallow. Orchard/Perennial: tree crop structure, intermediate NDVI/backscatter. Built‑up: high NDBI, low NDVI. Bare/Exposed: low NDVI/NDBI. Infrastructure/Transport: linear impervious features (OSM + morphology). Remove noise <0.1 ha; enforce min mapped patch 0.5 ha.

Validation and classifier targets
Training: stratified spatially‑balanced samples targeting 150–300 points per dominant class, 80–150 for rarer classes. Independent validation: 400–600 spatially independent points (target ≥50/class where feasible). Performance targets: overall accuracy ≥88%, major‑class user’s/producer’s ≥80% (rare classes ≥70%), report Kappa and F1 with 95% CIs. Spatial CV: 5‑fold spatially‑blocked CV, block ≈5 km, enforce ≥1 km separation between training and validation clusters.

Monte‑Carlo & sensitivity protocol
Vary (1) per‑pixel class by sampling classifier soft probabilities, (2) per‑ha valuation coefficients as log‑normal (default SD ~30% or literature‑informed), (3) InVEST/USLE parameters (ET/Kc ±20% normal, root depth ±25% normal, Z log‑normal ±30%, USLE C/P/K log‑normal ±50%), and (4) discount rate sampled across 2–5%. Ensemble size target n=1,000 (minimum 500 if compute‑limited). Apply Sobol global variance decomposition on representative subsets to apportion uncertainty.

Valuation sources and adjustments
Use local market & county agricultural statistics for provisioning, China‑specific peer‑reviewed meta‑analyses for regulating/cultural services (document sources; e.g., Xie et al. derivatives), IPCC/SCC and regional carbon prices for carbon. Normalize to a base year via CPI and PPP, present values in CNY and USD, and test discount rates 2%, 3% (baseline) and 5%. Compute marginal pixel value as DeltaValue_pixel = Σ_services (ΔService_pixel × UnitValue_service) with explicit pathway mapping to avoid double‑counting.

InVEST hydrology/sediment calibration & turbidity conversion
Calibrate Kc/ET coefficients, rooting depth, Z sediment‑delivery, USLE C/P/K and curve‑numbers. Derive SSC–turbidity regressions from paired turbidity probe readings and lab SSC bottle samples across flows (SSC = a × turbidity + b) and compute sediment load = SSC × discharge for calibration targets; report NSE, RMSE, bias.

Land‑change calibration, scenarios & resistance
Estimate transition matrices from two historical windows weighted by interval length; prefer Dinamica EGO for allocation; validate with Figure of Merit, Fuzzy Kappa, Lee‑Sallee, ROC/AUC and patch metrics. Translate stakeholder narratives into relative changes to transition probabilities (examples: Development +30–50% urban conversion within 2 km of roads; Conservation −30–70% in buffers). Publish base resistance table (example: forest=1, wetland=2, orchard=3, shrub=4, grass=5, irrigated cropland=10, rainfed=12, water=50, bare=80, infra/built=100) and aggregate multispecies resistance via weighted geometric mean; test ±50% in ensembles.

Sampling, ethics, timeline, leads and minimal budget
Biomass plots: 20–40 20×20 m per woody class; soil cores 5–10 per class (0–10, 10–30 cm; extend deeper where feasible). Household survey 300 minimum (400–500 preferred). Obtain IRB/consent, anonymization and data‑sharing agreements prior to fieldwork. Three‑month milestones: weeks 1–2 confirm EPSG/legend and data requests; weeks 2–4 finalize sampling maps and permits; weeks 4–10 field campaign and turbidity traps; weeks 10–14 lab analyses, mapping prototypes and model calibration; deliverables: legend, sample datasets, calibrated InVEST inputs, validation report and uncertainty ensembles. Assigned leads: Mapping — Remote Sensing Specialist; Modelling — Land‑Change Analyst; Valuation — Ecosystem Valuation Economist; Hydrology — Hydrology Lead; Socio — Policy Lead; Data Manager — GIS Specialist.
