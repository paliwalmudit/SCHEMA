Uncertainty representation and propagation
Treat encoder feature maps per spatial location as diagonal Gaussians with mean m(x,c) and log-variance s(x,c)=log v. Probabilistic output and loss

1) Sample-based heteroscedastic NLL: sample logits K times from N(μ_logits, Σ_logits), compute softmax per sample, average to obtain predictive class probabilities p̂, and train with cross-entropy NLL( p̂, y ). Backpropagate through samples with reparameterization.

Ensembles give strongest empirical fidelity; MC-dropout is cheapest and compatible with single-model training.

Calibration, regularization, and numeric safeguards

Theoretical properties and limitations
Diagonal Gaussian assumption is compact but ignores cross-channel and spatial covariance; analytic moment propagation is approximate and degrades with strong nonlinearities. Sampling reduces approximation error but increases cost. Evidential approaches may overfit if regularizers are weak. Ensembles approximate multimodal posteriors well but cost scales linearly with members.
