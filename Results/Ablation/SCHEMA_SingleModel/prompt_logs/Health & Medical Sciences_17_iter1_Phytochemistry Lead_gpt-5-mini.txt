Concise GLP acute and 28‑day NOAEL protocol (summary)
- Acute: OECD‑style single‑dose oral gavage at 300, 1000 and 2000 mg/kg with 3 rats/sex/dose, 14‑day observation, necropsy on survivors; clinical checks twice daily first 72 h then daily. Satellite PK cohort (n=3/sex) for 0.5,1,4,8,24 h sampling. Stopping: >20% weight loss, severe dyspnea, unresponsive state, uncontrolled bleeding.  
- 28‑day repeat dose: groups control, low, mid, high (provisional 50,150,450 mg/kg or adjusted after acute); n=10/sex/group with n=5/sex recovery groups. Toxicokinetics on day 1 and day 28 (serial sampling). Clinical chemistry: CBC with platelets, PT/aPTT, fibrinogen, D‑dimer, ALT, AST, ALP, bilirubin, creatinine, BUN, glucose, lipids, electrolytes, CRP. Histopathology: liver, kidney, heart, lung, spleen, thymus, bone marrow, GI tract, pancreas, adrenals, brain, gonads, lymph nodes and injection site. NOAEL defined as highest dose with no treatment‑related mortality, no clinically significant dose‑dependent changes in clinical pathology or organ histopathology and no >20% weight loss versus control. Owners: PK/Tox + Phytochemistry produce full protocol within 7 days.

Two‑phase herb–herb interaction strategy
- Phase 1 in vitro: run orthogonal assays (platelet aggregation LTA/Multiplate, thrombin generation CAT, NETosis MPO‑DNA) with 8×8 concentration matrices combining two extracts (concentrations spanning IC10→IC90). Analyze with isobolograms and Chou‑Talalay Combination Index (CI): CI<0.9 = synergy, 0.9–1.1 = additive, >1.1 = antagonism. Trigger fractionation when crude combo shows CI<0.9 in at least two orthogonal assays at concentrations achievable in vivo.  
- Phase 2 fractionation → in vivo: fractionate by solvent partition (hexane, EtOAc, n‑BuOH, aqueous) and test active fractions in targeted in vivo thrombosis models at doses matching achievable plasma exposures; prioritize fractions that reproduce synergy and have acceptable acute safety.

PK interaction assays and in vivo sampling
- In vitro panel: CYP inhibition (human CYP1A2,2C9,2C19,2D6,3A4 and rat analogs), time‑dependent inhibition, hepatocyte induction, UGT panel, transporters (P‑gp, BCRP, OATP1B1/1B3), microsomal stability, plasma protein binding.  
- In vivo crossover PK: each herb alone and the triherbal combo in rats with blood at pre,0.25,0.5,1,2,4,8,12,24 h; calculate Cmax, AUC, t1/2, CL/F and compare for >25% changes prompting mechanistic follow‑up. Include day 1 and day 28 sampling to detect induction.

FeCl3 carotid and IVC SOP locked parameters

- IVC stenosis: 90% stenosis using 30G spacer and 6‑0 ligature; euthanize at 48 h for thrombus weight/length. Owners: Rodent lead to circulate SOP PDFs within 7 days.
