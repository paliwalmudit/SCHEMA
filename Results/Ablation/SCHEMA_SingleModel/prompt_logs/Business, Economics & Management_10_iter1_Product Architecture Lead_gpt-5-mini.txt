Candidate metrics (definitions and rationale)
1. Structural Modularity Index (SMI): normalized modularity from a product-part graph (e.g., based on Louvain/Modularity Q or normalized cut) to capture separability of physical modules; range 0–1. Use multi-resolution detection to capture hierarchy.
2. Functional Entropy (FE): Shannon entropy of function assignments across modules to quantify functional decomposition spread; higher values indicate dispersed functions.
3. Functional Overlap Ratio (FOR): fraction of functions implemented in more than one module across variants; useful for redundancy vs. reuse tradeoffs.
4. Interface Standardization Score (ISS): weighted count of standardized interface types divided by total interface instances; combine backward/forward compatibility weights.
5. Coupling-Cohesion Vector (CCV): two-part metric where Coupling = average external dependency edges per module (normalized) and Cohesion = intra-module similarity (e.g., TF-IDF on functional descriptions or graph density). Report both values and their ratio.
6. Granularity Index (GI): mean and variance of module sizes (mass/cost/complexity) plus hierarchy depth; use Gini coefficient to capture size inequality.
7. Variability Index (VI): product configurational entropy based on option counts and constraint graph—effective configurational space size.
8. Reuse/Commonality Rate (RCR): percent of BOM items or modules common across a platform; track reuse frequency distribution.
9. Evolutionary Modularity Metrics: Module Stability Index (MSI) = 1 − normalized churn rate of module interfaces/definitions over time; Modularity Change Velocity (MCV) = rate of module merges/splits per release.

Composite and multi-scale constructs
- Modular Architecture Index (MAI): PCA- or SEM-derived composite of SMI, ISS, CCV, GI, and RCR with validated weights. Report at module-, product-, and platform-level.
