  - Covariates: porosity (volume fraction, connectivity from micro-CT), grain-size metrics (D10, D50, D90, sorting), cementation index, modal mineralogy, inclusion fraction.
  - Priors: weakly informative for H and Er grounded in literature ranges; informative priors for band thresholds (Low/Medium/High) anchored at provisional values (Low: H<1 GPa, Er<20 GPa; Medium: H≈1–3 GPa, Er≈20–50 GPa; High: H>3 GPa, Er>50 GPa) with uncertainty to allow site-specific revision.
  - Outputs: posterior distributions for H, Er, and band probabilities with credible intervals; performance metrics via Brier/CRPS for band assignment; nested leave-one-domain-out validation.

- Field-to-lab transfer function
  - Two-stage calibration: (1) map rebound/impact waveform features to lab H and Er; (2) map lab H/Er to band probabilities; incorporate domain- and sample-level random effects to capture site heterogeneity.
  - Target initial field-to-lab uncertainty: ~10–20% for H and ~10–25% for Er; design pilot to push toward ≤15% where feasible.

- Microstructure co-registration and data schema
  - Use a fixed coordinate system with fiducial markers visible across thin-section, SEM-EDS, and micro-CT; generate a domain registry (Domain A/B/C) linked to indentation targets with exact coordinates and photos.
  - SEM-EDS resolution: 0.5–1 µm in fine domains; 1–2 µm elsewhere; micro-CT voxel ≤10 µm; export domain-level descriptors and pmi (porosity metrics, pore size distribution, cementation index) attached to indent metadata.
  - Data schema: specimen.ID, domain.label, indent.ID, coordinates, H, Er, CVs, raw curves, imaging pointers (thin-section, SEM map, CT), knapping notes, and provenance metadata.

- Validation and reporting
  - Implement nested cross-validation with emphasis on domain transferability; report posterior predictive checks, calibration curves, and uncertainty coverage for H/Er and band classifications.
  - Provide a concise, machine-readable reporting template: raw curves, QC metrics, domain descriptors, H/Er with intervals, band probabilities, and knapping calibration notes.

- Deliverables for Meeting 2
  - Finalized protocol, signed pilot sample list, field SOP, lab bookings, data schema, and responsibility matrix; grant a concise one-page data-sharing template and a short interim report emphasizing uncertainty budgets and transfer performance.

These refinements maintain comparability across samples, emphasize uncertainty-aware interpretation, and embed ethical field practices while delivering a practical, collaboration-friendly path to meeting milestones.
