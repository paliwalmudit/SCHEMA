Pilot adjudication on 150 events with dual independent reviews, standardized training, calibration exercises, and a predefined timeline (reviews within 7 days, resolution within 14 days). Use a third senior adjudicator to resolve disagreements and log all decisions for CAPA.

Third, ensure robust event linkage and traceability. Maintain a persistent event_id linking accession_no, test_result, qc_log, maintenance/calibration history, reagent_lot, operator_id, EQA results, and raw instrument data. Publish the data dictionary and provide example rows to support auditability and RCA workflows.

Fourth, establish real-time instrument-level monitoring. Deploy control charts (u-chart, g-chart) and change-point detection to flag drift or shifts with automated, clinically informed alerts. Require immediate verification after instrument maintenance before result release, and implement defined escalation paths to technologist, supervisor, and clinician.

Fifth, finalize sampling and power planning. Provide analyte-specific power tables; for example, with 50,000 annual tests and a baseline high-severity rate of 2 per 1,000, a 20% relative reduction is detectable with 80–90% power. Target about 400 high-severity chart reviews to assess outcome differences. Pre-register primary endpoints and plan multiplicity control (hierarchical testing or FDR).

Sixth, plan missing data, bias, and causal inference. Predefine multiple imputation for covariates, inverse-probability weighting for selection bias, and sensitivity analyses for unmeasured confounding. Use DAG-based causal frameworks with propensity-score or case-crossover designs where feasible.

Seventh, outline ML safeguards and deployment. Develop temporally split, instrument-clustered datasets; report sensitivity, specificity, PPV, AUC, calibration, and SHAP explanations. Require an 8–12 week prospective pilot before production, with drift monitoring and recalibration every 6 months.

Eighth, governance and privacy. Obtain IRB/DUA approvals as appropriate, apply salted hashes with a secure linkage vault, RBAC, AES-256 at rest, and 7-year retention with full provenance logs.

Ninth, define KPIs and evaluation. Target median time-to-detection <4 hours, time-to-resolution <48 hours, CAPA on-time >90%, and a 25% reduction in high-severity events at 12 months, assessed by segmented regression and NB models.

Tenth, resource alignment and timeline. Confirm LIS access, finalize priority analytes and thresholds, and commence pilot adjudication and power-table publication. Maintain contingency buffers in ETL and data governance to assure timely delivery.
