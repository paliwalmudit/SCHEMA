Algorithmic architectures
- Phased Epoch-Based Decentralized UCB (D-UCB-PE): agents run synchronized epochs. - Contextual/Linear Decentralized Bandits: agents maintain local LinUCB estimates and periodically average parameter estimates via limited gossip (compressed updates); exploration bonuses adjusted for consensus error.

- Lower bounds: prove decentralization cost: any decentralized policy without global coordination incurs additional regret Ω(M log T) beyond single-agent lower bounds, and constant factor dependence on collision model and communication rate.
