- Baseline requirement: exchangeability of (Z1,...,Zn,Z_{n+1}) (i.i.d. is a special case). Split-conformal and standard jackknife+/CV+ guarantee finite-sample marginal coverage under exchangeability.  
- Weighted/proximity conformal: marginal validity holds only if the calibration sample plus test point are exchangeable after reweighting — equivalently weights must be proportional to the test-vs-train density ratio w(x) ∝ dP_test(x)/dP_train(x). If weights are incorrect, depend on Y, or learned from the same Y used for calibration, coverage can fail. - Exact jackknife+ requires leave-one-out predictors μ̂_{-i}(·) for all i (refitting n forests gives provable coverage). Practical RF approximation: compute per-tree predictions and for each i define μ̂_{-i}(x) as the average of predictions from trees for which training i was OOB (i.e., the tree did not include i in its bootstrap).
