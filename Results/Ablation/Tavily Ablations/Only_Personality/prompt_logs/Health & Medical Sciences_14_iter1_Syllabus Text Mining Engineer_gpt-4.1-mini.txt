All key usage is logged to support auditability.

Storage and retention policies define a tiered lifecycle: raw scraped documents retained transiently for 90 days, parsed structured records retained for three years to support reproducibility and re-review, and fully anonymized derivatives retained for ten years. Secure deletion SOPs generate immutable deletion manifests with audit logs. 

Published derivatives follow a stringent approval matrix: aggregate statistics and reproducible code under CC-BY license, fully anonymized datasets after passing re-identification assessments also under CC-BY, while quasi-identifying or sensitive OCR text is released only under Data Use Agreements with IRB oversight and secure environment requirements.

Provenance and audit trails are maintained via semantic versioning of dataset manifests, codebooks, and annotation logs with immutable change-logs capturing dates, authorship, rationales, and representative examples for every update, facilitating regulator audits and reproducibility. Low-confidence OCR and high-legal-risk items trigger human review quotas integrated into the CI/CD pipeline, overseen by the data manager.

Finally, IRB and legal integration entails a comprehensive scraping legality checklist verifying robots.txt compliance, institutional permission where feasible, documented copyright statements, automated takedown contacts and processes, and inclusion of informed consent templates and participant information sheets for interviews and surveys. This holistic design ensures that syllabus data collection, processing, analysis, and publication are aligned with Indian regulatory realities, institutional reputational safeguards, and international best practices for ethical research and data governance.

In summary, this integrated NLP pipeline and auditable data governance framework enable precise, scalable, and legally defensible analysis of AI ethics content across India’s computing curricula, supporting evidence-based policy recommendations and pedagogical advancements grounded in cultural and regulatory contexts.
