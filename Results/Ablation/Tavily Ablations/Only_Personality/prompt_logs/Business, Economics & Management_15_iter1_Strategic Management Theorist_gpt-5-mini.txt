Integrative theoretical framing: I propose the Purpose-Driven Renewal (PDR) framework that links corporate purpose to strategic renewal via three core mechanisms—sensemaking/identity alignment, resource mobilization/governance, and dynamic capability activation—each moderated by contextual and organizational contingencies. 1. Sensemaking/Identity Mechanism: Purpose clarifies what matters, shaping managerial and employee attention.
- Proposition 1: Higher purpose clarity positively affects the firm’s sensing capability, increasing identification of strategic change opportunities.

3. Dynamic Capability Activation Mechanism: Purpose provides motivational and cognitive scaffolding that facilitates recombination of assets.

4. Proposition 4: When purpose is highly normative and rigid, it increases persistence tendencies and reduces exploratory renewal, producing capability lock-in under low environmental dynamism.
