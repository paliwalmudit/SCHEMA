Harmonization rules: link records by geolocation + parcel ID; prioritize SIGPAC for cultivated parcel boundaries when SIGPAC vs REGA area difference ≤10%; if >10% flag for farmer confirmation and use survey-validated area. Use Catastro for legal property boundaries and REGA for declared exploited area and variety; AEAT/SABI for firm-level financial validation. Convert and store all units (ha, kg, L) with provenance flags.

Measurement-error bounds for simulations: assume yield per ha relative error 10–30% for survey/field measures and 5–15% for administrative (SIGPAC) yields; assume Brix instrument+sampling error ±0.1–0.5 °Bx (central ±0.2 °Bx). Use ICC priors 0.05–0.20 and temporal autocorrelation ρ between 0.2–0.6 when simulating clustered longitudinal DiD power envelopes.

Statistical corrections: pre-specify SIMEX and regression-calibration using validation data, mixed-effects measurement-error models, and latent-variable harmonization; where credible, use administrative measures as instruments and implement bounding analyses and permutation tests with cluster-robust SEs.

Validation sub-sample and protocol: reserve 10–15% of plots (minimum ~100 plots across ≥50 firms) for duplicate field measures (two independent samplers), duplicate lab assays, and repeated within-season and next-season re-sampling to estimate measurement-error variance, ICCs, and temporal autocorrelation for informed power calculations and bias correction.

QA, governance and ethics: produce SOP checklists, training modules, an annotated codebook, versioned pipelines, and data-sharing agreements with tiered access. Implement multilingual consent, robust anonymization for migrant workers, and participatory validation workshops. Pilot these procedures in 2–3 contrasting regions within 6 months to refine parameters before full rollout.
