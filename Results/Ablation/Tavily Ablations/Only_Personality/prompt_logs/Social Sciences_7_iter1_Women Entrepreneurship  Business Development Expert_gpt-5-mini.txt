1) Sample-size guidance and formulas

- Continuous revenue outcome: n_per_arm ≈ 2*(Zsum^2)*σ^2 / Δ^2, where Zsum=(Z1-α/2+Z1-β). Example: mean=$200, σ≈$300, with n=400 listings/arm, detectable Δ≈$60 (≈30%); with n=800 listings Δ≈$42 (≈21%). Use these formulas to produce exact MDEs after baseline variance is measured.
