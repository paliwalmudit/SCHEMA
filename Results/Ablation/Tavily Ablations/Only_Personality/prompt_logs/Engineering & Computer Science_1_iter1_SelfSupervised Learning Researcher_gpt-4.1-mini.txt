Key randomized physics parameters (units, ranges, recommended distributions and sampling)
- Young’s modulus E: 20–40 GPa, recommended prior: Normal(μ=30 GPa, σ=5 GPa) truncated to [20,40]. Sample with Latin Hypercube (LHS) to cover tails. Typical source ranges per ACI/Eurocode material catalogs.

- Tensile strength ft: 2–5 MPa, Normal(μ=3.5 MPa, σ=0.8 MPa) truncated to [2,5].
- Fracture energy Gf: 40–200 N/m, Lognormal(μln≈4.0, σln≈0.5) to capture long tail.
- Density ρ: 2200–2500 kg/m3, Uniform.
- Interface cohesion/friction: cohesion 0.1–2.0 MPa (Uniform), friction μ=0.3–0.8 (Uniform).
- Sampling guidance: for a 10k dataset use stratified LHS across geometry × defect_type × major physics bins (E, Gf, ft); ensure ≥100 samples per defect_type × geometry cell for statistical power.

Sensor forward models, impulse responses, sampling and noise (lab-validated recommendations)
- Acoustic Emission (AE): model each micro-crack event as a source-time function s(t) convolved with Green’s function G(r,t) for elastic waves. Use vP≈3500–4500 m/s, vS≈1900–2600 m/s. Sensor impulse: bandpass (100 kHz–1 MHz) approximated by Gaussian-modulated sinusoid. Sampling rate 5 MHz, SNR range 0–30 dB. Noise model: colored Gaussian + rare impulsive spikes; validate against lab pencil-lead breaks.
- Ultrasonic A-scan: 1D reflectivity model from linear wave equation; reflectivity R=(Z2−Z1)/(Z2+Z1), Z=ρc. Transducer impulse: Gaussian-modulated sinusoid with center f0=100 kHz–1 MHz depending on probe, bandwidth 50–80%. Sampling rate 10–50 MHz. Attenuation α(f) exponential with frequency; SNR 10–40 dB. Add coherent ringdown and electronic noise.
- GPR: use FDTD or analytic layered reflection model; relative permittivity εr in 4–14, conductivity σ small; center freq 200 MHz–2 GHz depending depth/resolution. Time binning 1–10 ns. Antenna impulse by Ricker/Gaussian derivative. SNR 5–30 dB with clutter and surface ringing.
- Validation plan: record calibration pulses, pencil-lead breaks, and known reflectors in lab specimens to tune impulse-response parameters and noise statistics.
