Metric pipeline and statistical assumptions
- Whitening and PCA: remove outliers (>5 MAD), regularize with ε = 1e-6 · trace(C)/d, compute C = UDU^T, whiten with W = D^{-1/2}U^T, retain components explaining up to 95% variance (capped at 128). Validate Gaussianity with Henze–Zirkler or Mardia; if violated, fall back to nonparametric KL or Wasserstein distance. Report both raw units (D_KL in nats, Euclidean distances) and normalized percentiles.
- Surprise and normalization: report raw S_raw and normalized S_norm via predictive entropy scaling; pre-register 95th percentile noise-floor thresholds from corpora resamples.
- Critic training and calibration: partition human labels into Train (60%), Calib (20%), Eval (20%) with no overlap between Eval artifacts and training data. Isotonic calibration with cross-validated mapping; track reliability using reliability diagrams and ECE; specify fallback to Platt/temperature scaling if calibration fails.
- Primary endpoint and analysis: primary endpoint is proportion of artifacts with C≥T (T=0.6) under lexicographic rule; report alternative aggregations and rank correlations. Use mixed-effects models with random intercepts for rater and artifact and fixed effect for operator type; pre-register multiple-testing corrections (FDR) and per-domain contrasts.
