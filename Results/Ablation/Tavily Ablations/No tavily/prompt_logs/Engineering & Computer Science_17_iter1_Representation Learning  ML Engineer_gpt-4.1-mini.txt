Time–Frequency Analysis Lead: "Summary and one-page math spec (concise)

Goal: provide an explicit STFT/multitaper measurement model, units/scaling table, principled inference switching rules, seeded synthetic JSON schema, identifiability thresholds justification, and short project assignments to run the baseline demo.

(a) Measurement/observation model (closed-form approximation)
Let x(t)=∑_k A_k(t) exp(iφ_k(t)) + ε(t) with f_k(t)=φ_k'(t)/(2π). The continuous STFT with window w(·) at time t0 and frequency f is S_x(t0,f)=∫ x(τ)w(τ−t0)e^{−2πi f(τ−t0)}dτ. For a single slowly-varying AM–FM mode around t0, Taylor-expand A_k and φ_k to second order to obtain the stationary-phase approximation:
S_k(t0,f) ≈ A_k(t0) e^{iφ_k(t0)} Ŵ(f−f_k(t0)) e^{iψ_corr},
where Ŵ is the Fourier transform of w and ψ_corr encodes phase-curvature terms from φ_k''(t0). Consequently the expected TF-bin energy (multitaper-averaged) is approximated by
E[|S_k(t0,f)|^2] ≈ A_k^2(t0) |Ŵ(f−f_k(t0))|^2 ⊛ G_{f'}, 
where ⊛ denotes broadening from instantaneous-frequency curvature (chirp-rate) f'≈φ_k''/(2π) producing an effective Doppler spread proportional to |f'|·T_w^2. For K tapers T_p, multitaper expectation is the average over taper spectra |V̂_p|^2. Measurement nonlinearity arises because observations are magnitudes/energies (quadratic) and sum across components; for mild nonlinearity UKF suffices, while severe multimodal posteriors (crossings) require RBPF.
