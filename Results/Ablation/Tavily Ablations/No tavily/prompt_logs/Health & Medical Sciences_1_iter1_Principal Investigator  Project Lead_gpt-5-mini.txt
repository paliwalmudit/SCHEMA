Model formulation

- Impose a spatially aware prior on z to encourage contiguity of salient regions. Let the prior p(z) be Gaussian with mean m0 and a precision that encodes neighborhood structure: p(z) ∝ exp(-1/2 (z−m0)^T (τI + κL) (z−m0)), where L is the patch-grid graph Laplacian. This yields an implicit Gaussian Markov random field promoting smooth attention maps.
- Use an amortized variational posterior q(z|X) = N(μ(X), diag(σ^2(X))) produced by a small encoder conditioned on patch features. The encoder computes μ_i and logσ_i for each patch i.
- Define a differentiable MIL classifier that uses the expectation of the attended features. Let r = ∑_i a_i f_i (or use a_i f_i with stochastic sampling for robustness). The bag probability is p(y=1|X) = σ( w^T φ(r) ), where φ can be a shallow MLP on the aggregated feature r.
- Optional discrete variant: use Bernoulli a_i with a Gumbel-Softmax relaxation for a_i during training, enabling a more interpretable sparse selection while remaining end-to-end trainable.

Inference and training objective
- Optimize the evidence lower bound (ELBO) with the bag label likelihood:
  ELBO = E_{q(z|X)}[ log p(y|X, z) ] − KL( q(z|X) || p(z) ).
