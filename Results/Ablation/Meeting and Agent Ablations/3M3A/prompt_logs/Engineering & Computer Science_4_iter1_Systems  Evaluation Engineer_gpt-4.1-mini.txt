Mathematical hypergraph operators

- Alternative row/column normalizations: A_ne_rc = D_v^{-1/2} I W_e C_e^{-1/2} (symmetric surrogate).
- Laplacian variants:
  - Node Laplacian L_N = I W_e C_e^{-1} I^T - D_v (unnormalized diffusion operator L = D_v^{-1} I W_e C_e^{-1} I^T).
  - Normalized hypergraph Laplacian (Zhou et al.): L_sym = I W_e C_e^{-1} I^T D_v^{-1} - I_N.
