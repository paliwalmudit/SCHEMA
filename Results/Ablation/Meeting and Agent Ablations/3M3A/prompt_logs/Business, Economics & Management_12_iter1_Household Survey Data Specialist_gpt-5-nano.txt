Demographic ageing alters the distribution of individuals across life-course stages and household types, changing both the incidence of income sources (labor, pensions, transfers, remittances) and the unit of economic sharing (individual vs household). - How much of observed cross-country and temporal change in income inequality in CEE is explained by shifts in age structure versus shifts in household composition?

- How do pension system features (replacement rates, contributory vs universal elements, indexation) and migration/remittance flows modify the relationship between ageing, household structure, and inequality?
- Are trends gendered or cohort-specific (e.g., disadvantaged female cohorts with weak contributory histories)?
