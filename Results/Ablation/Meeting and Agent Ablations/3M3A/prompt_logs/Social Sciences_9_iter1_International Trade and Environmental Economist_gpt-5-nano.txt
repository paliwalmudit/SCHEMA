- Legal triggers: map to observable indicators such as P(B<0.5BMSY) thresholds, biomass declines, and IUU-detection rates; quantify FP/FN under WTO/PSMA-compatible rules.
- Social metrics: labor by region, household welfare, income distribution, and gender-disaggregated effects; derive from census and surveys with regression decomposition.

HPC, deliverables, and governance
- HPC plan: embarrassingly parallel runs on clusters/cloud; checkpoint per OM; estimate 5k–20k CPU hours; 2–5 TB storage; reproducible release with containerized environments.
- Immediate outputs: data dictionary and API specification; calibration/validation plan; MSE design; data-access agreements; a schedule with clear milestones and leads. Schedule regular integration workshops to align interfaces and update priors.
