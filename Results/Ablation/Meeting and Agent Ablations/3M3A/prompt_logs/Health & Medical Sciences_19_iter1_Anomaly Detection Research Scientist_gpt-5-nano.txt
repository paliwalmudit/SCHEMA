- Matching: perform greedy bipartite matching between predicted and gold spans per document, maximizing overlap area (token-level intersection). A pair is eligible only if label sets intersect. Score overlap ratio = |pred ∩ gold| / |pred ∪ gold|.
- Credit rules: exact match (overlap=1.0) → full credit; partial match (overlap ≥ 0.5) → fractional credit = overlap_ratio; overlap < 0.5 → no match.
- Multi-label/overlap: allow multiple labels per span; matches computed per (span,label) pair; during bipartite matching each gold-span can match multiple predicted labels but at most one predicted span per label (use label-aware matching).
- Severity-weighted F1: assign weights w(severity) (default: minor=1, major=3, critical=5). Compute weighted TP = Σ w * credit over matched (span,label) pairs, weighted FP = Σ w * (1 - credit) over unmatched predictions, weighted FN = Σ w over unmatched gold.
