Numerical concordance rules: concordant = overlap of 95% credible intervals across independent dates; provisionally concordant = overlap within ±1σ; discordant = no overlap at ±1σ. In the latter case, triggers include resampling on the horizon, applying an additional independent dating method, or auditing taphonomy and micromorphology; unresolved horizons will be flagged and treated with reduced weight in dispersion inferences.

QA/QC and contamination controls: OS L single-grain thresholds include 50–150 accepted grains per sample, dose-recovery 1.00 ±10%, recycling within 10%, recuperation <5%, and explicit overdispersion checks with finite-mixture modelling. Ring-test participation will include Oxford Luminescence Lab, Cologne, and Bern with blind standardized samples. Contamination SOPs for sedaDNA and palaeoproteomics will specify clean-room class, blanks, independent replication, and authentication thresholds (peptide-spectrum match FDR ≤1%, endogenous DNA thresholds).

Data management: CIDOC-CRM aligned metadata in JSON-LD, with mandatory fields and documented provenance, deposited in Open Context or tDAR; ChronoBank/OxCal outputs for chronology data; sequence reads in ENA/NCBI; proteomics in PRIDE; embargo periods and DOIs clearly defined.

Pilot-go/no-go criteria: proceed to full excavation when ≥3 concordant independent horizon dates are achieved, target proxies preserved in ≥50% of samples, and taphonomic metrics indicate primary deposition. I will deliver Monte Carlo scripts, a JSON-LD metadata template, and ring-test protocols for Meeting 2, and welcome feedback on prioritizing sites and any lab partners.
