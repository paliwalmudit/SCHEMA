  "Abstract": "We address analog circuit link prediction: deciding whether a candidate connection between ports exists or would materially affect analog performance, and estimating that effect. We propose GNN-ACLP, a method that (1) applies subgraph-based embeddings to candidate port neighborhoods for fine-grained, port-level link prediction, (2) integrates SPICE-derived small-signal features via a differentiable sparse solver to inject physics-aware signals, and (3) uses an LLM-powered netlist conversion tool to normalize netlist dialects into a canonical graph format. We contribute a labeled SpiceNetlist dataset of 775 transistor-level circuits across seven families and ten component classes. The main contribution is an end-to-end, reproducible pipeline combining subgraph GNNs, differentiable small-signal physics, and netlist-format augmentation to improve both classification precision and impact-regression accuracy. Expected outcomes: higher Precision@K and lower MAE on analog impact metrics versus topology-only and non-physics baselines, improved netlist compatibility across formats, and a reproducible benchmark suite for analog link prediction.",
