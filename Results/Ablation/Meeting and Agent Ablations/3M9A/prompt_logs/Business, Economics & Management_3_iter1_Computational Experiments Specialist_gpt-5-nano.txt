2) Reservation feasibility (necessary/sufficient statement and scheme)
Define integer assignments z_{ij}∈{0,1} with sum_j z_{ij} ≥ p (p=F+1) and reserve w_{ij}=d_i z_{ij}/p (equal split). A necessary and sufficient feasibility condition for no over‑commitment under this scheme is existence of z,y satisfying for all j: (1/p)∑_i d_i z_{ij} ≤ s_j y_j and z_{ij} ≤ y_j. Proof sketch: equal split ensures each client keeps one surviving share after up to F failures; the per‑facility reserved sum equals the load the facility must serve in the worst case and is bounded by s_j y_j by the inequality, so no facility is overcommitted. This yields a simple sufficient/constructive reservation rule; alternative schemes (prioritized reservations or per‑client caps) can be obtained by replacing equal split with weighted splits and tightening the per‑facility bound accordingly.
