This balances diminishing returns on uncertainty reduction with compute cost; a pilot of N = 100–200 draws will be used to compute the running mean and CI of key area and transition statistics and stop expanding N when successive increases change median area estimates by <0.5% and 95% CI widths stabilize (owner: Data Scientist & Uncertainty Analyst).

Staged compute plan and stopping rules: pilot classifier training and 200-draw allocation ensemble runs on cloud GPUs (one A100-equivalent for classifier training, ~48 GPU-hours) and multi-core CPU nodes for allocation models (CA‑Markov/CLUE‑S/Dinamica/agent each run on 16–32 cores). Estimate ~100–200 core-hours per 100 realizations; storage ~4 TB for all rasters and intermediate products. Stage 1: N=100 pilot; Stage 2: N=1,000 operational; Stage 3: targeted upscaling if variance not stabilized. Stopping rules: area/transition variance stabilization and CI width targets or a max core-hour budget (owner: GIS & Spatial Data Engineer).

Ensemble weighting formula: compute per-model skill z-scores for metrics {Kappa, Figure‑of‑Merit, landscape‑metric divergence, AUC_change}. Normalize metrics to z_{ik}, apply weighted sum S_i = Σ_k α_k z_{ik} (initial α = {0.4,0.3,0.2,0.1} but tunable). Convert to weights with softmax and governance scaling: weight_i = (exp(S_i) * g_i) / Σ_j (exp(S_j) * g_j), where g_i ∈ [0,1] is stakeholder governance trust for model i. This yields normalized, interpretable weights and allows sensitivity testing across α and g (owner: Land Use Change Modeler + PI for governance input; Data Scientist for implementation).

Spatial resampling to preserve autocorrelation: use a two-step approach—spatial block bootstrap with block size = 2–3× empirical semivariogram range derived from pilot data, combined with conditional simulation via a categorical Markov Random Field (MRF) conditioned on per‑pixel posteriors to preserve patch geometry and class contiguity; tune MRF smoothness parameter to replicate observed patch‑size distributions (owner: Remote Sensing Analyst + Data Scientist).

Parcel/household inputs and behavioral model specs: require annual parcel-level: land area, tenure type, crop/fruit type, planting density, irrigation access/abstraction, inputs (fertilizer), yields, off-farm income share, household demographics, credit access, parcel coordinates; temporal resolution annual with seasonal optional fields; behavioral rules: discrete choice or logistic transition probabilities conditioned on economic returns, tenure, and labor; calibration targets: observed transitions (2000–2015) and village-level yield/income trends; missing data handled by multiple imputation (MICE) with informative priors where available (owner: Socioeconomic & Policy Analyst + Land Use Change Modeler).

Variance decomposition and EVPI mapping: use Sobol global sensitivity indices computed on low-cost emulators (Gaussian process or random-forest metamodels) to apportion variance to classification, parametric, and structural sources, and compute component-level EVPI to prioritize data collection (owner: Data Scientist & Uncertainty Analyst).

Hydrology/ES comparability: match inputs at 30 m and monthly/annual time steps; run InVEST water-yield with same LULC and meteorology, and calibrate SWAT and HEC‑HMS with identical DEM, soils, and LULC; calibration window 2000–2015, validation 2016–2022; report NSE, KGE, PBIAS for flow and percent bias/error for sediment (owner: Hydrology & Watershed Modeler).

Validation sample sizes: endorse ~1,000 field points with per-class minima: common classes ≥70, ambiguous/mosaic 120–180, rare classes targeted ≥50 but increased by targeted sampling; power confirmed via pilot Wilson CI calculations (owner: Field Survey Coordinator).

MOUs, consent, and data tiers: prepare three tiers—public aggregate products, restricted research datasets (anonymized), and sensitive household-level data under controlled access with signed MOUs and consent templates; PI and Socioeconomic Analyst to finalize legal language (owner: PI + Socioeconomic Analyst).

Biodiversity resistance and HSI: initialize resistance values from literature-based defaults and expert elicitation, then locally calibrate via targeted occupancy/camera‑trap and pollinator transect surveys at 30–50 sites; update resistance surfaces and HSI parameters iteratively (owner: Landscape Ecologist).
