- Fit RF on training data; compute OOB predictions for each training point.
- Conformity score = |y_i − ŷ_i^{OOB}|.

- Guarantee: finite-sample marginal coverage under exchangeability.

2) Normalized (heteroskedastic-adaptive) conformal
- Estimate local scale σ̂(x) with RF (regression on |residual| or via quantile forest spread).
- Use conformity score = |y_i − ŷ_i| / σ̂(x_i).
- Use quantile of normalized scores and form interval ŷ_new ± q·σ̂(x_new).
- Practical: use OOB σ̂, OOB ŷ to avoid leakage.
- Benefit: intervals adapt to heteroskedastic noise; still enjoys marginal coverage.

3) Conformalized Quantile Regression (CQR) with quantile forests
- Fit two conditional quantiles q̂_α/2(x), q̂_{1−α/2}(x) with quantile forests.
- Compute conformity scores as max(q̂_α/2(x_i) − y_i, y_i − q̂_{1−α/2}(x_i)).
- Calibrate using split-conformal to correct coverage and reduce interval width relative to symmetric intervals.

- For a test x, define a local calibration set via forest proximity (shared leaves across trees) or leaf membership.
- Compute weighted empirical quantile of calibration residuals using proximity weights.
