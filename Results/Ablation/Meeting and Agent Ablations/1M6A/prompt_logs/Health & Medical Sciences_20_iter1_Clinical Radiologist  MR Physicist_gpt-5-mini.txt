Priority clinical use-cases (top 3, with rationale)

Ground-truth labels and annotation standards
- Gold standards: histopathology and molecular markers (when available) for tumor classification; follow-up imaging and stability/progression as temporal truth; digital subtraction perfusion/CT or conventional angiography for vascular correlates when relevant; biopsy-sampled regions for molecular labels (IDH, MGMT).
- Annotation types: voxel-wise segmentation masks, bounding boxes, lesion-level labels (enhancing/non-enhancing), structured severity scores (e.g., ASPECTS for stroke adapted to MRI), and radiology report-linked DICOM SR.

XAI evaluation framework (clinically meaningful metrics)
- Fidelity: perturbation-based fidelity (input masking — does explanation removal change prediction) and quantitative localization (IoU and lesion-wise sensitivity between heatmap thresholded regions and segmentation masks).

- Clinical plausibility: checklist-based rating by neuroradiologists scored for alignment with known imaging signs (e.g., does the model highlight enhancement, diffusion restriction, edema separately?), and case-level trust rating (Likert scale).
