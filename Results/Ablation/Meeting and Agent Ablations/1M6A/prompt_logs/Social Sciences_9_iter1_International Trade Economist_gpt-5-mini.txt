Key research questions

- What is the role of non-tariff measures (SPS, traceability, ecolabels) in changing market access costs and compliance incentives for small-scale versus industrial fleets?  
- Do trade provisions reduce or shift illegal, unreported, and unregulated (IUU) fishing by changing profits and supply chains?

Methods and empirical strategy

- Partial-equilibrium trade-supply modules (species- or commodity-specific) linked to dynamic bioeconomic models (Gordon-Schaefer type or age-structured) to translate price signals into effort, catch, and biomass trajectories.  
- Welfare and distributional analysis to decompose effects across processors, exporters, fishers, and consumer markets.  
- Sensitivity and uncertainty analysis across elasticity priors and biological parameters.
