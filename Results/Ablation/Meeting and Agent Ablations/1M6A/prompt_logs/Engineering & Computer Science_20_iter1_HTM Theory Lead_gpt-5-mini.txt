I propose adding a biologically plausible, hardware-friendly "reflex memory" subsystem that operates in parallel with standard HTM Spatial Pooler (SP) and Temporal Memory (TM). The design uses two-timescale plasticity, eligibility traces gated by a neuromodulatory signal, and a sparse associative representation compatible with SDRs.

Architecture and representation:

- Dual-representation: reflex entries use slightly higher density (k_rel) than cortical SDRs for robustness under noisy inputs, but remain sparse relative to full dimensionality to preserve capacity and energy efficiency.
- Interface to TM: reflex outputs can directly bias cell depolarization or trigger motor/inhibitory outputs, bypassing TM prediction when reflex confidence exceeds a threshold.

Learning rules and dynamics:
- Fast plasticity: reflex synapses obey a rapid Hebbian-like rule with neuromodulatory gating: Δw_fast = α_fast * pre * post * M - β_fast * w_fast, where M represents a global salience/novelty signal (e.g., prediction error or simulated acetylcholine/dopamine). α_fast >> α_slow.

- One-shot potentiation option: when prediction error is extreme, allow one-shot strengthening up to a cap, enabling immediate reflex responses to novel but critical stimuli.
