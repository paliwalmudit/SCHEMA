Key components are (1) an instance encoder (CNN or patch-transformer), (2) a probabilistic attention module that outputs a distribution (mean, variance) over attention logits for each instance, (3) a smoothing prior implemented as a graph- or Laplacian-based regularizer acting on attention weights, and (4) a variational training objective (ELBO) combining bag-level classification likelihood, KL term, and smoothness penalty.

Model/attention formulation:
- Encode each instance xi to hi via a pretrained backbone (ResNet/ViT). Project hi to attention latent ai ~ N(mu_i, sigma_i^2) where mu_i = f_mu(hi), log sigma_i = f_sigma(hi). Sample ai via reparameterization: ai = mu_i + sigma_i * epsilon.
- Convert sampled logits to weights via tempered softmax: w_i = softmax(ai / tau). Use temperature tau to control sparsity.

- Bag prediction: y_hat = classifier( sum_i w_i * g(hi) ) where g may be identity or separate instance embedding head.
- Objective: minimize expected negative log-likelihood over bag labels + beta * KL[q(a)||p(a)] + Loss_smooth, using Monte Carlo samples per bag for gradients.
