Intervention overview and theoretical basis:
RHT combines narrative therapy, life-review, and meaning-centered psychotherapy with guided use of historical research (archival materials, community histories) to help clients situate personal stories in broader historical and social contexts. The approach aims to increase narrative coherence, agency, and a sense of continuity and significance, which are empirically linked to improved psychological well-being.

Core components and session structure:
- Format: Individual therapy, 8–10 weekly sessions (50–60 minutes), with optional family session and a culminating "legacy product" (written narrative, audio recording, or curated scrapbook).
- Modules: 1) Engagement and baseline assessment; 2) Life timeline and salient events; 3) Introduction to historical contextualization and collaborative research; 4–5) Guided integration of archival/context materials with personal narrative; 6) Meaning-making exercises (values, purpose, reframing); 7) Consolidation of revised narrative and coping strategies; 8) Closure, legacy presentation, and relapse prevention.
- Activities: Timeline mapping, co-research assignments, meaning-focused writing, narrativization exercises, and community/ancestral linking.

Pilot study design:
- Aim: Test feasibility, acceptability, safety, and preliminary efficacy on meaning and well-being.
- Sample: 40 adults (diverse backgrounds), randomized 1:1 to RHT vs. psychoeducation active control or waitlist. Pilot powered for feasibility; not definitive efficacy.
- Inclusion/exclusion: Adults with subclinical to moderate distress; exclude acute suicidality, unmanaged psychosis, or severe cognitive impairment.
- Measures (pre, post, 3-month FU): Meaning in Life Questionnaire (MLQ), Ryff Psychological Well-Being scales, WHO-5, PHQ-9, GAD-7, narrative coherence coding, Client Change Interview for qualitative acceptability. Feasibility metrics: recruitment, retention, adherence, time spent on co-research.
- Analyses: Descriptive feasibility stats, within- and between-group effect size estimates, thematic analysis of qualitative data.

- Fidelity monitoring: Session checklists, audio-recording 20% of sessions for independent fidelity rating, and client-rated adherence/acceptability surveys.
