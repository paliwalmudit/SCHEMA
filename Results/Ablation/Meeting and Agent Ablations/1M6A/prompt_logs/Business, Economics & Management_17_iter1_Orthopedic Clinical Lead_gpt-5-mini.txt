Inclusion and exclusion criteria (practical, clinically meaningful)
- Include adults (≥18 years) undergoing elective primary total hip or total knee arthroplasty discharged to home with a planned pharmacologic VTE prophylaxis regimen (oral DOAC or subcutaneous LMWH) for ≥14 days.  
- Include patients able to provide informed consent and complete the scale in the study language (or validated translation) and who are cognitively intact (no delirium/dementia precluding self-report).  
- Exclude patients receiving therapeutic anticoagulation for other indications (chronic A-fib, recent VTE), those discharged to skilled nursing facilities or inpatient rehab, patients with major bleeding disorders or contraindications to prophylaxis, and those undergoing revision or complex bilateral procedures if they substantially change standard discharge pathways.

Key scale domains and example items (keep scale brief for feasibility)
- Perceived risk/severity: “I believe I am at risk of blood clots after surgery.”  
- Perceived benefit: “Taking my prescribed medicine will significantly reduce my chance of a clot.”  
- Perceived barriers: cost, side effects, fear of bleeding, injection discomfort, pharmacy access.  
- Self-efficacy: confidence to take medication correctly (including injectables).  
- Practical cues/support: presence of caregiver support, reminders, prior instruction from clinician/pharmacist.  
- Prior experience/attitudes: prior anticoagulant use, needle aversion, general medication beliefs.  
Aim for 6–10 items to balance psychometric robustness and clinical feasibility.

Clinically meaningful endpoints and measurement
- Primary validation endpoint: adherence (objective when possible) measured by electronic pillbox/patch monitors, pharmacy refill records, or structured pill counts; define nonadherence as <80% of doses taken during prophylaxis period. - Secondary clinical endpoints: symptomatic VTE within 90 days, major bleeding (ISTH criteria), readmissions related to VTE/bleeding, patient-reported adherence and satisfaction.
