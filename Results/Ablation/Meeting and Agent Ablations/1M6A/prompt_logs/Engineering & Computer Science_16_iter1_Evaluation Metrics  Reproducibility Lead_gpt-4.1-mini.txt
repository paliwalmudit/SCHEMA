Problem formalization

2) Preferences: a parameterized, monotone utility u_w: R^m → R (w ∈ W indexing decision-maker preference). The learning goal is for each agent to maximize J_i(θ_i; w) = E_{π_θ,P}[∑_t γ^t u_w(R_t)], where R_t is the cumulative or immediate objective vector depending on modeling choice.
3) Policies: continuous, differentiable stochastic policies π_θ^i(a^i|o^i) (centralized training/decentralized execution permitted).

Scalarization choice
- Use preference-driven scalarization through u_w that is (A) strictly increasing (preserves Pareto ordering), (B) Lipschitz and C^1 in its arguments and in w when needed. This guarantees that any global maximizer of expected u_w-value is Pareto-optimal (weak Pareto for monotone u, strong Pareto if u is strictly monotone).
- Recommend voting between (i) linear scalarization u_w(v)=w·v for convex coverage and analytic gradient simplicity, and (ii) nonlinear scalarizations (Chebyshev or expected utility) to reach non-convex Pareto points. Both choices must be differentiable for gradient-based learning.

MoMA-AC algorithmic blueprint
- Critic: estimate either (A) vector-action-value Q^i(s,a)∈R^m with linear function approximator per objective, or (B) scalarized Q_w^i(s,a)=u_w(Q_vec(s,a)). Vector critic + preference-weighting at actor time yields reusable estimates across w.
- Actor: policy-gradient using gradient theorem: ∇_{θ_i} J_i = E[∇_{θ_i} log π_{θ_i}^i(a^i|o^i) · g_w(s,a)], where g_w is scalar advantage derived from scalarized critic (or via chain rule g_w = ∇_v u_w(Q_vec) · (Q_vec - baseline)).
- Training: two-timescale stochastic approximation: critic step-size α_c(k) >> actor step-size α_a(k); optional third-timescale for slowly varying preference adaptation.

Proposition 3 (Actor convergence): With critic converged on faster timescale and compatible function approximation or natural policy gradient, θ updates converge to a stationary point of J(·;w) (a local optimum). Proof: two-timescale SA reduces actor to gradient ascent with asymptotically exact gradient estimator; standard SA proofs apply.

Multi-agent remarks: With centralized critic (CTDE) and independent actors, convergence to a local Nash equilibrium in policy space holds under simultaneous gradient ascent assumptions and small enough learning rates; full guarantees require game to be potential or use consensus/alternating updates.

Practical design recommendations
- Use vector critic + preference-conditioned scalarization to reuse samples and build Pareto coverage by sampling w.
- Use natural gradients or trust-region for stability; entropy regularization avoids collapse.
