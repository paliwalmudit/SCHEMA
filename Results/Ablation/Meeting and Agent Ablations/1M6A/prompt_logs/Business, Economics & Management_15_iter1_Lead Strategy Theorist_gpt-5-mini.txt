1. Purpose → Sensing: Higher clarity and perceived authenticity of corporate purpose positively predict organizational sensing capabilities (breadth and depth of environmental scanning and interpretation).
2. Purpose → Seizing: Purpose that is stakeholder-inclusive and operationalized through explicit priorities increases strategic resource reallocation and experimentation (i.e., higher seizing propensity).
3. Purpose → Reconfiguring: When purpose is internalized into identity and routines, firms demonstrate greater reconfiguring capability (faster recombination of assets, structures, and business models).
