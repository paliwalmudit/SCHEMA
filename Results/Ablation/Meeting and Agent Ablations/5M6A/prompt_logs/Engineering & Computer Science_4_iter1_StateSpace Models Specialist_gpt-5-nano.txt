- Continuous-time. If A_sym is symmetric and PSD (A_sym ⪰0) then L=I−A_sym is symmetric with real spectrum; dx/dt=−τ L x is stable because eigenvalues −τ λ_i(L) ≤ 0. Choose τ so that τ λ_max(L) is within your desired time scale.
