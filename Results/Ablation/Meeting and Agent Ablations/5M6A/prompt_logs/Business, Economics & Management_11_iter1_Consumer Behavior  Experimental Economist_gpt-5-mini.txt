Diagnostics to be preregistered include heaping histograms, residual correlations with covariates, attenuation-factor comparisons, RMSE against validation subsamples, and Monte Carlo recovery under alternative error DGPs. Owner: Microdata & Econometrics Lead; deadline: 2026-09-15.

I formalize an IV causal DAG: global commodity/packaging-price shocks and freight/tariff shocks → firm input costs → packaging/size decisions → observed unit values/CPI, with demand, marketing, and quality as potential confounders controlled by rich fixed effects and hedonic covariates. Pre-registered IV tests include first-stage diagnostics (distribution of F-statistics, Kleibergen-Paap, partial R2 with median F>10 decision rule), placebo lead tests, Hansen J overidentification, event-study pre-trends, and falsification on non-traded goods and marketing proxies. Owner: Identification Lead; deadline: 2026-10-01.

Owner: Data Lead; deadline: 2026-08-31.

Owner: Validation Lead; deadline: 2026-09-30.
