  - Predefine ecological go/no-go triggers: if posterior probability of detrimental stock status exceeds 0.5 for two consecutive assessment periods, or if key stocks fail pre-registered ecological benchmarks, trigger adaptive sampling, model refinement, or pilot scale-back.

- Deliverables, ownership, and preregistration
  - Assign clear owners for ecological dashboards, priors, validation plans, and go/no-go criteria; publish a preregistration document detailing estimands, priors, MDEs, convergence/ESS criteria, and sensitivity plans.

- Reproducibility and governance
  - Enforce containerized workflows, data dictionaries, and staged synthetic data releases; include an ecological appendix with thresholds and sensitivity analyses.

These refinements ensure the ecological core is transparent, testable, and policy-relevant, with explicit triggers that keep the pilot scientifically defensible and adaptable to findings.
