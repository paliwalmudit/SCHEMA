Temporal/separable strategies

Complex wavelets for alignment and phase cues
Include a dual-tree complex wavelet transform (DT-CWT) option to extract local phase responses for robust motion/shift estimation. Use phase correlation between complex subbands as a lightweight alignment cue that is often more robust to speckle and intensity changes than intensity-based flow.

Compress memory values in the wavelet domain: project LL bands to compact channels (1×1 conv) and heavily quantize or sparsify high-frequency bands with L1 penalty.
