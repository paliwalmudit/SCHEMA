Social cohesion is a multi-level, dynamic condition in which durable patterns of positive social relations (trust, reciprocity, and cooperation) across and within groups, perceived institutional legitimacy and fairness, inclusive access to rights and resources, and shared civic commitments collectively enable peaceful coexistence, collective problem-solving, and resilience to shocks. This definition foregrounds both micro-level relations and macro-level structures, and recognizes that cohesion can coexist with, or be undermined by, strong within-group ties that exclude outsiders.

Hypothesized dimensions (theoretical boundaries)
1. Horizontal relations: bonding and bridging ties, intergroup trust, frequency and quality of intergroup contact.  
2. Vertical relations: trust in public institutions, perceived legitimacy, responsiveness and accountability.  
3. Shared belonging and civic identity: felt sense of national, regional, or local belonging and endorsement of common rules.  
4. Inclusion and equity: perceived and actual access to services, economic opportunities, and political representation across groups.  
5. Civic engagement and collective action: participation in associations, public deliberation, and cooperative provision of local public goods.  
6. Norms and social capital: norms of reciprocity, tolerance for diversity, and informal enforcement mechanisms.  
7. Resilience and conflict mitigation: capacities to manage grievances peacefully, social repair after violence, and readiness to cooperate under stress.

Operationalization and measurement strategy
- Multilevel latent construct approach: measure individual attitudes/behaviors (surveys, vignettes, network data) and aggregate to community/region while retaining macro indicators (service delivery, inequality, conflict incidence).  
- Core micro indicators: generalized trust, interpersonal trust across salient cleavages, willingness to marry/work/live across groups, participation rates, perceived fairness, reported neighborly help, and responses to standardized vignettes on cooperation.  
- Core macro indicators: public service reliability, corruption indices, Gini coefficient, ethnic fractionalization, violent incidents, social protection coverage, spatial segregation metrics.
