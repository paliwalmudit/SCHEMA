Operational definitions (general formulas).
- Novelty N(x) = normalized distance in perceptual embedding: N(x) = d(ϕ_perc(x), μ_train)/σ_train where d is Mahalanobis or cosine and ϕ_perc is domain-tuned.  
- Conceptual novelty Nc(x) = distance in semantic or causal latent space: Nc(x) = d(ϕ_sem(x), nearest_concept_cluster)/scale.  
- Surprise S(x) = information gain under forward model M: S(x) = KL[p_M(next|context,x) || p_M(next|context)] or practical proxy prediction-error magnitude.  
- Value V(x) = E[y|x] from critic C(x) with epistemic uncertainty: V(x) = C_μ(x), uncertainty = C_σ(x).  
- Validity R(x) = Boolean or graded penalty combining syntactic and semantic checks; final objective L = αV + βN + βcNc + γS − λR_penalty.

R(x) domain-specific checks and adversarial probes.
- Poetry: syntactic (tokenization, meter consistency), semantic coherence (LM perplexity thresholds), novelty-vs-plagiarism (n-gram overlap), meaning checks (semantic entailment probes).

- Music: MIDI range, instrument feasibility, rhythmic metrical validity, harmonic rule heuristics.
