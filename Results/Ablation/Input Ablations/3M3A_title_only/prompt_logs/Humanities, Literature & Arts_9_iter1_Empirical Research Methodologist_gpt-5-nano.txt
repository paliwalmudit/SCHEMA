Document data‑sharing agreements, DPAs, and SCCs; specify when in‑country hosting is preferred and under what conditions cross‑border transfers are authorized with equivalent protections.

Data governance: DAC and appeals SOPs
- DAC composition: 5–7 members including two researchers, one data‑protection officer, one independent ethicist/civil‑society representative, and one data security expert. Appeals panel: 3 independent members. Develop SOPs with clear criteria, recusal rules, decision timelines (initial decision within 4 weeks), logging, and annual transparency audits.

Pilot design, nonresponse, and missing data
- Pilot with 30 institutions and 60–120 respondents; implement oversampling and personalized outreach, small incentives, and two reminders plus phone follow‑ups. Plan weighting by institution type/region and multiple imputation under MAR with sensitivity analyses for MNAR.

Breach response and retention
- Detection within 24–48 hours, containment actions within 24 hours, notification to affected parties within 72 hours, and governance/DP breach reporting per APPI. Retain identifiers only as long as necessary; separate key files with strict access controls; default retention 5 years post‑publication.

 preregistration, reproducibility, and qualitative integration
- Pre‑register on OSF; containerized analysis pipeline; redacted outputs for public release. Triangulate qualitative and quantitative findings via joint displays, codebook with intercoder reliability κ ≥ 0.70, and report following SRQR/STROBE guidelines.

Operational staffing and budget
- Recommend FTEs: PI 0.15, project manager 0.5, statistician 0.2, data manager 0.3, legal/APPI reviewer 0.15, translator 0.2, field admin 0.4. Budget lines: participant incentives, translation/back‑translation, in‑country hosting, independent audit, DPIA/legal review, stakeholder workshops, and dissemination.
