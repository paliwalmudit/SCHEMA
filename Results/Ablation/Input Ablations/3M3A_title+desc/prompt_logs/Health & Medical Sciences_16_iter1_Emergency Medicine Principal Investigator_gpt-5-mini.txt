Research question and rationale
Investigate whether the admission serum anion gap (AG), particularly albumin‑corrected AG, predicts elevated serum lactate (primary threshold ≥2.0 mmol/L; secondary ≥4.0 mmol/L) among adults diagnosed with sepsis in the ED who are not in septic shock. Study design
Phase 1: multicenter retrospective cohort (electronic health record data) for derivation and internal validation. Phase 2: prospective observational validation at selected EDs to confirm real‑time applicability.

Case definition and inclusion/exclusion
- Inclusion: adults (≥18) presenting to ED with suspected infection and organ dysfunction consistent with Sepsis‑3 (increase in SOFA ≥2 or pragmatic ED SOFA/qSOFA‑based adaptation), with serum electrolytes and lactate measured within the first 3 hours.
