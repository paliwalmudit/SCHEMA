Probabilistic ML Architect: "Below I resolve the priority gaps (KL/prior numerics, numerical strategy, scaling/fallbacks), lock the baselines+ablation, prescribe a preregistered evaluation/tests plan, and give a compact training/stability recipe, interpretability thresholds, reproducibility & deployment artifacts.

1) Closed‑form KL and stable computations
For q(z)=N(μ,Diag(σ^2)) and p(z)=N(0,Σ_p) with precision Λ := Σ_p^{-1} = λ_I I + λ_L L, the ELBO KL term has the closed form
KL(q||p) = 0.5 [ trace(Λ Σ_q) + μ^T Λ μ − K + ln det(Σ_p) − ln det(Σ_q) ],
where Σ_q = diag(σ^2) and K is number of instances. Using det(Σ_p)=det(Λ^{-1}) we write
KL = 0.5 [ ∑_i (λ_I + λ_L deg_i) σ_i^2 + μ^T (λ_I I + λ_L L) μ − K − ln det(Λ) − ∑_i ln σ_i^2 ].
Implementation notes: compute trace(Λ Σ_q) cheaply via ∑_i (λ_I+λ_L deg_i)σ_i^2; compute μ^T Λ μ by sparse matvec tmp = λ_I μ + λ_L (L @ μ) then μ·tmp; compute −∑ ln σ_i^2 directly.

2) Log‑det and large sparse precision numerical strategies
- Moderate N (up to ~50k nodes, memory permitting): sparse Cholesky (CHOLMOD/SuiteSparse) to compute logdet(Λ) = 2∑ log diagonal(Cholesky factor). This is accurate and fast when fill‑in is manageable.
