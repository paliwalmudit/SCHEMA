Sampling loci and documentation
- Microdrill plan: limit sampling to existing breaks, hidden surfaces, or restoration waste; document coordinates with a standardized map, angular orientation, and minimum-disturbance zones. MicroCT parameters: voxel size 10–20 μm, 16‑bit stacks; photography checklist and barcode-based chain-of-custody. Deliverables: annotated sampling map, formal sampling limits (≤20 mg per assay; cumulative ≤50 mg/accession), and imaging datasheets.

Inter-lab validation and replication
- Inter-lab design: minimum two AMS labs and at least three biomolecular labs; blind duplicates for each assay; proposed n=30 per assay with ≥8 known positives/negatives as a power anchor. Acceptance: AMS dates within lab error; inter-lab concordance for molecular profiles ≥85%; Bland–Altman summaries for method biases. Replica wear testing: draw weights 30–70 lbs, conditioning 20±2°C/50% RH, ~2000 cycles, n≥8 per variant; quantify interface-docused wear via micro-CT/SEM.

Permits, provenance, schedule, and data management
- Provide templates for sampling consent, export/import permits, embargo options, and stakeholder liaison schedules; assign data stewardship to ensure FAIR compliance (ISA-Tab, Darwin Core, DOIs, versioning). Deliverables: permit templates, liaison contact list, and a detailed 12–18 month timeline with milestones.

Diffusion hypotheses operationalization
- Use Bayesian posteriors and cross-validated molecular clocks to test Near Eastern-origin vs pan-Eurasian diffusion; declare falsification criteria (e.g., lack of concordant direct dates or inconsistent molecular signals across sites) and report where data robustly support or refute each scenario.
